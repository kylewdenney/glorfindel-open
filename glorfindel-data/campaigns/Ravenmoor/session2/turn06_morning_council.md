As they neared the chapel, Isolde's voice trembled with urgency, beseeching Father Vane to help them seal the malevolent force that plagued their village.

The miller's daughter had proven an elusive source of information, but Vera knew that she held secrets that could help them banish the malevolent force that haunted Ravenmoor.
