As the first light of dawn pierced through the twisted, gnarled branches above, the party found themselves standing at the edge of an abyss. The eastern well of Ravenmoor, a place shrouded in mystery and dread, loomed before them. Dorian crouched at its stone lip, his eyes fixed intently on the scratch marks that marred its surface – deep gouges, each one bearing the unmistakable impression of human fingernails, pressed downwards rather than upwards, suggesting the frenzied desperation of at least four different people. The water below lay dark and still, a perfect mirror to the eerie dawn light that danced upon its surface, lending it an unsettling life-like quality.

Vera knelt beside Dorian, her eyes closed as she let her Detect Magic flow through her. A 19 on her Arcana check (Arcana +5) revealed the magic below was not the necromantic force they had initially feared – it was compulsion. Something deep within the well was calling to them, a siren song that sent a shiver down Isolde's spine as she recognized the sensation from one of the ancient rites Father Vane had spoken of the previous night. Her face paled, and her grip on her silver holy symbol tightened until her knuckles turned white.

The party stood at the brink of a mystery that seemed to stretch out before them like an endless chasm, their hearts pounding with a dread born of the unknown. The village of Ravenmoor watched from a distance, its shadowy figures seeming to whisper dark secrets into the wind as they observed the strangers who had dared to approach the well. The air was thick with anticipation and fear, a palpable sense that something malevolent lurked beneath the surface, waiting for the opportune moment to strike.
