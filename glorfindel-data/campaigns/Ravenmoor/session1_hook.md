# Session 1 - The Hollowbrook Investigation

Battered by the relentless drizzle and shrouded in a thick fog, you find yourselves standing before the dilapidated village of Hollowbrook, nestled deep within the desolate Ashfen Marshes. The once-prosperous town has fallen into ruin, its buildings crumbling and overgrown with weeds.

You gather at The Crossed Road, the only means of entry or exit, where an ancient iron cross marks the junction. Locals leave flowers here as offerings to the dead. As you approach the village, a feeling of unease settles over you — something is not right about this place.

The villagers have been disappearing one by one, and it falls upon your party to investigate these strange occurrences and uncover the secrets buried beneath Hollowbrook's haunted past. The Aldwick Inn, run by Marta Voss, will serve as your temporary home during this harrowing ordeal.

As you enter the inn, you find yourself surrounded by locals who are wary of strangers and speak in hushed tones. They have seen their friends and neighbors vanish without a trace, leaving only whispers of spectral sightings behind. You must be cautious as you delve deeper into the mysteries of Hollowbrook, for something malevolent lurks within this accursed village.

The rules will test your party's skills in investigation, social encounters, and combat as you face encounters with supernatural entities. Fear saves will be crucial to overcome the chilling dread that permeates Hollowbrook. As you uncover the truth behind the missing villagers, you must confront the darkness that has plagued this village for far too long.

Your character sheets are as follows:
- **Sister Isolde Carrow:** A fallen cleric who carries a silver holy symbol she no longer believes in. Her divine magic still works — which terrifies her. Dry wit, compulsive caretaker, terrified of being responsible for another death.
  - Relevant traits: Medicine +5, Religion +4, Insight +4. Advantage on saves vs. undead fear effects. Disadvantage on Wisdom saves when she witnesses suffering.
- **Dorian Ashgrove:** A disgraced investigator dismissed for pursuing a case no one wanted solved (the Holloway disappearances, 1891). Carries a leather notebook with obsessive detail. Drinks too much gin. Brilliant, paranoid, occasionally right about things no one else notices.
  - Relevant traits: Investigation +6, Perception +4, History +4. Has a network of informants (1d4 days to get a reply). Disadvantage on social checks when sober.
- **Emmett Grave:** A graverobber turned archaeologist who calls himself an archaeologist. Knows twelve ways to open a locked crypt and has used all of them. No moral qualms. Extremely cheerful about death.
  - Relevant traits: Sleight of Hand +5, Thieves' Tools +5, Arcana +3, History +3. Can identify undead type on a DC 12 Arcana check. Immune to the Frightened condition (he's seen worse).
- **Vera Nighthollow:** A witch who lives on the edge of law and the edge of the forest. Knows herbalism, folk magic, and things her grandmother told her never to repeat. Speaks to animals (they don't like what they tell her). Distrusted by respectable people, sought out when they're desperate.
  - Relevant traits: Nature +5, Arcana +5, Animal Handling +4, Deception +4. Prepared: Detect Magic at will. Can brew potions (1 hour, herbalism kit, DC 12 Arcana). Disadvantage on checks in cities, advantage in wilderness/ruins.
