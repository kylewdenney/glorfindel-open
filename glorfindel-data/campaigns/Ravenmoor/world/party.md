A former healer of St. Aldric's order who lost her faith after failing to save a dying child. Carries a silver holy symbol she no longer believes in. Her divine magic still works — which terrifies her. Dry wit, compulsive caretaker, terrified of being responsible for another death.

| Medicine | +5 |
| Religion | +4 |
| Insight | +4 |

- Disadvantage on Wisdom saves when witnessing suffering
- No special modifier on Cosmic Dread Saves (Intelligence)

## Vera Nighthollow — Witch

Lives on the edge of the law and the edge of the forest. Knows herbalism, folk magic, and things her grandmother told her never to repeat. Speaks to animals (they don't like what they tell her). Distrusted by respectable people, sought out when they're desperate.

**Abilities**
| Skill | Modifier |
|-------|----------|
| Nature | +5 |
| Arcana | +5 |
| Animal Handling | +4 |
| Deception | +4 |

**Special Rules**
- Advantage on Cosmic Dread Saves (Intelligence) — her grandmother's knowledge
- Disadvantage on checks in cities; Advantage in wilderness/ruins
- Detect Magic at will (does NOT detect the Thing from Below — senses only absence)
- Can brew potions (1 hour, herbalism kit, DC 12 Arcana)
