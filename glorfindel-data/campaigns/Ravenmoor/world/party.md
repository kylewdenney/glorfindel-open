# The Party

## Sister Isolde Carrow — Fallen Cleric
A former healer of St. Aldric's order who lost her faith after failing to save a dying child. Carries a silver holy symbol she no longer believes in. Her divine magic still works — which terrifies her. Dry wit, compulsive caretaker, terrified of being responsible for another death.
*Relevant traits: Medicine +5, Religion +4, Insight +4. Advantage on saves vs. undead fear effects. Disadvantage on Wisdom saves when she witnesses suffering.*

## Dorian Ashgrove — Disgraced Investigator
Former crown investigator, dismissed for pursuing a case no one wanted solved (the Holloway disappearances, 1891). Carries a leather notebook with obsessive detail. Drinks too much gin. Brilliant, paranoid, occasionally right about things no one else notices.
*Relevant traits: Investigation +6, Perception +4, History +4. Has a network of informants (1d4 days to get a reply). Disadvantage on social checks when sober.*

## Emmett Grave — Graverobber turned Archaeologist
Calls himself an archaeologist. Knows twelve ways to open a locked crypt and has used all of them. Surprisingly knowledgeable about burial customs, ward-markings, and funerary rites. No moral qualms. Extremely cheerful about death.
*Relevant traits: Sleight of Hand +5, Thieves' Tools +5, Arcana +3, History +3. Can identify undead type on a DC 12 Arcana check. Immune to the Frightened condition (he's seen worse).*

## Vera Nighthollow — Witch
Lives on the edge of the law and the edge of the forest. Knows herbalism, folk magic, and things her grandmother told her never to repeat. Speaks to animals (they don't like what they tell her). Distrusted by respectable people, sought out when they're desperate.
*Relevant traits: Nature +5, Arcana +5, Animal Handling +4, Deception +4. Prepared: Detect Magic at will. Can brew potions (1 hour, herbalism kit, DC 12 Arcana). Disadvantage on checks in cities, advantage in wilderness/ruins.*
