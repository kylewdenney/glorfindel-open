# Key NPCs — Ravenmoor

## Marta Voss — Innkeeper
Mid-50s, stout, iron-grey hair. Runs the Aldwick Inn with terrifying efficiency. Cousin to the Voss family, which she leverages constantly. Knows everything that happens in the village. Will only talk after the third drink, and only in generalities. Secretly: she's been poisoning the well water (not maliciously — she thinks the iron sulfate keeps the "echoes" away; she's partially right).

## Father Dorian Vane — Priest of St. Aldric
Gaunt, 60s, trembling hands. Officiated the funerals of the forty-three miners and has been officiating Ravenmoor's dead ever since. Smells of incense and something underneath the incense. Knows the old rites — the ones that aren't in the official liturgy. Helpful if the party earns his trust (DC 16 Persuasion or DC 13 with evidence of the supernatural). Secretly: he performed a sealing rite on the mine doors eight months ago. It took something from him. He doesn't sleep anymore.

## Casimir Voss — The Missing Count
Aldric III's son, 32, reportedly last seen at his father's funeral. Locals say he inherited his father's "moods." Three servants quit the week after the funeral. No one has gone up to the manor to check. The iron gates are closed. Sometimes a light is on in the east tower.

## Lena Marsh — The Miller's Daughter
19, lives alone in the house beside the abandoned Millhouse. Refuses to leave. Says her father is "still there, just different." She leaves food out for him. It disappears. She describes him as "quieter now, but he doesn't forget her birthday." Her father drowned in the mine flood a century ago — she is clearly not nineteen. She doesn't know this.

## Old Tomas — The Gravedigger
Speaks in fragments. Has been gravedigger here for longer than anyone can remember. Carries iron nails in his pockets. Knows every grave by feel. Will warn the party not to disturb the forty-three. "They asked for it. Some of 'em got it." Won't elaborate.
