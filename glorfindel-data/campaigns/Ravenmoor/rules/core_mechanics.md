# Core Mechanics — Ravenmoor Gothic Horror System

## Skill Checks
All checks use 1d20 + relevant modifier vs. a Difficulty Class (DC).
- **Easy**: DC 8
- **Moderate**: DC 12
- **Hard**: DC 16
- **Extreme**: DC 20

**Advantage**: Roll 2d20, take higher.
**Disadvantage**: Roll 2d20, take lower.
**Critical Success**: Natural 20 — exceptional outcome, extra narrative detail.
**Critical Failure**: Natural 1 — something goes wrong, not always catastrophically.

## Contested Checks
When two characters oppose each other: both roll, highest wins. Ties go to the active party.

## Proficiency Bonus: +3 for all player characters.
