# Cosmic Horror — Ravenmoor Beyond the Veil

## The Threshold
Ravenmoor's hauntings were always gothic — local, comprehensible, rooted in grief and guilt.
What has risen from the mine is not. It predates the village, the marshes, the human species.
It has no name in any living language. It does not notice the party the way a predator notices prey.
It notices them the way a sleeper notices a fly on their face.

This changes the rules.

---

## Cosmic Dread (separate from Dread Points)

When a character directly encounters the Thing from Below — its shadow, its voice, its geometry, its implications — they make a **Cosmic Dread Save** (Intelligence save, not Wisdom). The mind, not the nerves, is what breaks.

| Encounter | DC |
|-----------|-----|
| Evidence of its existence (impossible geometry, wrong mathematics) | DC 11 |
| A fragment of its body or emanation | DC 15 |
| Its attention (it has noticed you specifically) | DC 19 |
| Direct perception of its true form | DC 23 |
| Understanding what it is and what it means | DC 27 |

**On failure:** Gain 1 Cosmic Dread Point (CDP). See below.
**On success:** The character compartmentalises. They can act, but they know something new that cannot be unknowed. Record it.

---

## Cosmic Dread Points (CDP)

CDP accumulates separately from the standard Dread Track. It does not heal with rest.

| CDP | Effect |
|-----|--------|
| 1 | The character occasionally sees angles that aren't there. No mechanical effect yet. |
| 2 | Disadvantage on Insight. The faces of people no longer read as faces first. |
| 3 | Once per session, the DM may describe one true thing about the universe. The character believes it. |
| 4 | The character must make a DC 14 Intelligence save to take a violent action against the Thing or its servants. Not from cowardice — from the dawning suspicion that it doesn't matter. |
| 5 | Dissolution. The character understands that identity is a local phenomenon. They can still act, but their goals have changed. Work with the DM on what they now want instead. |

**CDP never decreases naturally.** It can only be reduced by:
- A Ritual of Forgetting (requires forbidden materials, DC 19 Arcana, removes 1 CDP — the character forgets something else instead)
- Death and return (the character loses all CDP but also loses their name for 1d4 sessions)

## Forbidden Knowledge

Some information about the Thing should not be sought. Characters who actively research it — reading the mine records in full, translating the markings on the sealed door, asking Vane what he really saw — gain **Fragments**.

Each Fragment grants a mechanical benefit AND a CDP.

| Fragment | Benefit | Cost |
|----------|---------|------|
| Its True Name (partial) | +3 to saves against its servants | 1 CDP |
| The Geometry of Its Passage | Can detect its proximity (DC 10 Intelligence, 10 min) | 1 CDP |
| What It Wants | Advantage on one action to deny it, once | 2 CDP |
| What It Is | Immunity to its fear effects | 3 CDP |

Characters can choose not to acquire Fragments. Ignorance is a defensive strategy.

## The Unreliable Senses

At 3+ CDP, the party cannot fully trust their perceptions when near the Thing.

Once per scene, the DM may declare a **Veil Check** (no roll — just a declaration). The DM describes something. It may be real. It may not. Characters at 3+ CDP do not automatically know which.

To verify perception: DC 14 Intelligence save. Success: you know. Failure: you act on what you saw.

Emmett is not immune to this (he has seen worse, but not *this*).

## Confronting the Incomprehensible

The Thing cannot be killed with swords. It cannot be banished by St. Aldric's rites.
It can be:
- **Delayed** — the flooding of the mine sealed it once. A similar act could buy decades.
- **Confused** — it perceives through mathematics. Destroy its landmarks and it loses the thread.
- **Fed** — it wants something. Giving it the wrong thing costs something. Giving it the right thing is worse.
- **Named** — its True Name (complete) binds it to a location. One character must hold the Name in their mind. See CDP 5.

These are not solutions. They are the closest thing available.

## Vera's Position

Vera's grandmother told her things. Some of them were about *this*.

Vera has Advantage on all Cosmic Dread Saves. She also starts with 1 CDP — she already knew, in the way folk knowledge knows things, that something like this exists.

Her Detect Magic does not work on the Thing. What she senses instead: a silence where magic should be. A hole in the world shaped like something old.

## The Mine Door

The iron doors are stamped with warnings in three languages. The third language is not from any known culture.

The rite Father Vane performed sealed the doors eight months ago. It cost him sleep.
A second rite would seal it more completely. It would cost more than sleep.
The complete rite — the one in the untranslated text — would seal it permanently.
No one who reads the complete text has ever confirmed what it would cost.
