# Dice and Abilities — Ravenmoor Gothic Horror

## Core Mechanic
All checks: **1d20 + relevant modifier vs. Difficulty Class (DC)**.

Advantage: Roll 2d20, take higher. Disadvantage: Roll 2d20, take lower.
Critical Success: Natural 20. Critical Failure: Natural 1.
Proficiency Bonus: +3 for all player characters.

## Difficulty Classes

**Skill checks:**
- Easy: DC 8
- Moderate: DC 12
- Hard: DC 16
- Extreme: DC 20

**Investigation:**
- Obvious clue: DC 8
- Hidden clue: DC 13
- Expertly hidden: DC 17
- Social (general information): DC 12
- Social (secrets): DC 16

**Fear Saves (Wisdom save):**
- Unsettling: DC 10
- Disturbing: DC 13
- Terrifying: DC 16
- Soul-shaking: DC 19

**Cosmic Dread Saves (Intelligence save — the Thing from Below only):**
- Evidence of existence (impossible geometry): DC 11
- Fragment of its body or emanation: DC 15
- Its attention upon you directly: DC 19
- Direct perception of its true form: DC 23
- Understanding what it is and what it means: DC 27

## When to Call for a Roll
Call a roll when the outcome is uncertain and failure has meaningful consequences.
Do NOT call a roll for trivial actions, automatic successes, or pure narrative moments.

Output format (one line only):
  ROLL|CharacterName|ABILITY_NAME|1d20+N|DC|what it determines

If no check is needed: NO_ROLL
