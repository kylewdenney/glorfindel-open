# Fear, Dread, and Sanity — Gothic Horror Rules

## Fear Saves
When characters witness supernatural horror, they make a **Fear Save** (Wisdom save).
- **DC 10**: Unsettling — shadow moves wrong, sounds from a sealed room.
- **DC 13**: Disturbing — clear evidence of undead, violent unnatural death.
- **DC 16**: Terrifying — face-to-face with an active supernatural threat.
- **DC 19**: Soul-shaking — the void looks back, direct contact with the Horror.

**On Failure**: The character gains 1 Dread Point and must roleplay fear for 1 round (flee, freeze, or scream).
**On Success**: No mechanical effect, but good roleplaying of composure earns +1 to the next Fear Save.

## Dread Points
Each character has a Dread Track (0–5).
- **1 Dread**: Disadvantage on Insight and Perception while in Ravenmoor.
- **2 Dread**: Disadvantage on all social checks (haunted look, jumpy behaviour).
- **3 Dread**: Must make a DC 12 Wisdom save to willingly enter dark/enclosed spaces.
- **4 Dread**: Hallucinations — the DM may describe things that aren't there (once per session).
- **5 Dread**: Broken — the character cannot act offensively until another party member spends an action to snap them out of it.

**Recovering Dread**: A full night's rest in a safe, lit place removes 1 Dread Point. Magical healing removes 1 on a DC 14 Medicine/Religion check.

## Isolation Penalty
If a character is alone (no allies within 30 feet) in Ravenmoor at night: **Disadvantage** on all Fear Saves.

## The "Echo" (Seeing the Dead)
When a character sees an Echo (a lingering ghost-impression of the dead), they must make DC 10 Fear Save. Emmett is immune. Vera has advantage.

## Pressing On Through Fear
A character who fails a Fear Save may spend their action to **steel themselves**: DC 14 Wisdom save. On success, they act normally but gain another Dread Point.
