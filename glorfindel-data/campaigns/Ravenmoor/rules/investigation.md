# Investigation and Discovery — Gothic Horror Rules

## Active Investigation
**Searching a location**: DC varies by how well-hidden the clue is.
- Obvious (visible but overlooked): DC 8
- Hidden (concealed, requires looking): DC 13
- Expertly hidden (disguised, buried, coded): DC 17
- Roll Investigation (Intelligence) or Perception (Wisdom) — player chooses, DM decides which fits.

**Time cost**: Basic search 10 minutes. Thorough search 1 hour. Searching at night in poor light: Disadvantage.

## Clue Tiers
Every location has tiered clues:
1. **Surface clue** — found on any successful check, or automatically if the party spends 10+ minutes.
2. **Hidden clue** — requires DC 13+ or specific approach (Emmett on crypts, Vera on wards).
3. **Deep clue** — DC 17+, or only accessible with specialist knowledge.

## Gathering Information (Social)
Talking to locals: **DC 12 Persuasion** for general information. **DC 16** for secrets.
- Penalty: Locals in Ravenmoor are suspicious of outsiders. -2 to Persuasion until the party has been in town 24 hours.
- Bonus: Offering drinks at the inn gives Advantage. Father Vane responds to religious knowledge.

## Research (Books/Records)
Ravenmoor has a parish registry (at the chapel) and mine records (stolen from the Manor, now in the inn's back room).
- **Parish Registry**: DC 10 History to find relevant entries. Contains deaths going back 200 years.
- **Mine Records**: DC 13 Investigation. Reveals what the miners broke through before the flood.

## Vera's Detect Magic
At will. Reveals active magical auras within 30 feet. Suppressed magic (wards, seals) shows as a dim glow — DC 14 Arcana to interpret what it is.
