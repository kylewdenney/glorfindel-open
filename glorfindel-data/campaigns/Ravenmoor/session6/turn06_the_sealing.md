As Dorian emerged from the mine shaft, the oppressive silence of Ravenmoor enveloped him like a shroud. The rain had ceased, and the once murky waters that coursed through the village streets now lay still, mirroring the eerie calm that hung over the cursed village. The flickering lanterns of the Aldwick Inn cast their ghostly glow upon the desolate landscape, the spectral light dancing off the weathered stones of the crumbling buildings.

Vera stood in the village square, her eyes fixed on the fading paper clutched tightly in Casimir's hand. The name that had once been etched upon it had all but vanished, leaving only a ghostly imprint that seemed to whisper secrets long lost to time. She could feel the presence of the entity lingering in the air, its malevolent energy still resonating within the very marrow of Ravenmoor.

Isolde, her heart heavy with the weight of her faith and her failure, knelt by Father Vane's side as he stood guard over the sealed mine shaft. The fallen cleric could not help but feel a sense of relief wash over her as she watched the entity dissolve, but it was quickly replaced by an overwhelming dread that gnawed at her very soul. She knew that this was far from over—the darkness that had been unleashed upon Ravenmoor would not so easily be banished.

Emmett, ever the pragmatist, stood apart from the others, his eyes scanning the village with a keen gaze. The graverobber could feel the weight of the unknown bearing down on them, and he knew that their work was far from complete. With a steely resolve, he resolved to face whatever horrors lay ahead, determined to uncover the truth behind Ravenmoor's curse once and for all.

As the party stood in the silent village, the oppressive weight of the darkness that had been lifted lingered in the air like a specter, threatening to consume them all should they falter in their resolve. The night was young, and the shadows cast by the flickering lanterns seemed to dance with malevolent intent, taunting the party as they prepared to face whatever lay ahead.
