The drizzling rain pelted down on Sister Isolde Carrow as she disembarked from her carriage at the Aldwick Inn, a forlorn and decrepit building that seemed to have been carved directly out of the very soul of Ravenmoor. The inn stood as a grim testament to the village's cursed history, its dark wooden walls and decaying roof creaking in time with Isolde's racing heart. The air was thick with the smell of damp earth and rotting wood, a haunting perfume that seemed to seep into her very being. As she stood there, shivering beneath the cold embrace of the marshland night, she couldn't shake the feeling that she was not alone. She glanced around, her eyes straining to pierce the gloom, but all she could see were the twisted shadows cast by the feeble torchlight from the inn's windows. With a deep breath, Isolde stepped towards the door and entered the Aldwick Inn. Within, she found Dorian Ashgrove, a man of disheveled appearance and haunted eyes who seemed to fit the village like a glove. He eyed her warily, his hand never leaving the leather-bound notebook that he clutched tightly in his fingers. Isolde approached him, her resolve steeled by the cold winds that blew through the inn's cracked windows. She extended a trembling hand, introducing herself and explaining why she had come to Ravenmoor. Dorian regarded her for a moment, his eyes narrowing as he considered her words. Then, with an audible sigh, he agreed to speak with her, if only for a short while. Isolde's Charisma check of 13 allowed her to command the necessary respect from Dorian, and she began to pry information from him about the mysterious disappearances that had plagued the village in recent weeks. However, as they spoke, a sudden wave of fear washed over Isolde, causing her to roll a 20 on her Wisdom save against fear effects. The room seemed to close in around her, and she could feel the weight of Ravenmoor's dark history bearing down upon her. With an effort, she shook off the sensation and focused once more on Dorian's words, determined to uncover the truth behind the village's cursed existence.
