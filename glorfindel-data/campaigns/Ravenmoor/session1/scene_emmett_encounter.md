# Scene: Emmett's Encounter with Echo (Session 1) — **RAVENMOOR**,

In the dead of night, under the oppressive veil of a drizzling rain, Emmett Grave ventures out alone from the desolate village of Ravenmoor. The chilling wind whispers through the decaying trees, and the eerie silence is broken only by the distant howls of wolves.

With each step he takes deeper into the Ashfen Marshes, the atmosphere grows heavier with dread. Suddenly, a spectral figure appears before him - the Echo of a long-dead miner, its ethereal form flickering in and out of visibility.

Emmett, immune to fear as he is, merely observes the ghostly apparition. However, Vera Nighthollow, feeling her witch's senses tingle, detects a strong magical aura emanating from the scene (DC 14 Arcana). Her keen eyes scrutinize every detail, trying to decipher the secrets hidden within the spectral figure.

The village of Ravenmoor holds many mysteries, and tonight, Emmett's solitary encounter with Echo is only the beginning...
