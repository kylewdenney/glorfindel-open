# Session 1 - Scene 1: The Arrival in Hollowbrook's Embrace

As the stagecoach jolts to a halt, the passengers are greeted by an oppressive chill that seems to seep from the very soul of this desolate village. The damp, decaying scent of Hollowbrook hangs heavy in the air, a haunting reminder of the eerie, foreboding atmosphere that envelopes them.

The coachman, grizzled and weary, eyes the party with suspicion. The mysterious letters they received three weeks prior have painted a target on their backs, and he's hesitant to let them off without payment. As Elara, Thorne, and Vex step down onto the muddy ground, they must now rely on their wits and charm to navigate this tense social encounter.

The ominous darkness that cloaks Hollowbrook seems to swallow the coach as it rumbles away, leaving the adventurers alone in a place where lives are at stake—more than the ones still breathing. Their mission is just beginning, and the secrets of this cursed village await their discovery.

*Elara gains advantage on fear saves due to her sorcerous abilities.*
*Thorne potentially benefits from his Brave trait if he is playing as a Hobbit, granting him advantage on saving throws against being frightened.
