# Hook: Hollowbrook's Haunting Whispers

As the rain-soaked wind whispers through the decaying trees of Hollowbrook, you and your fellow travelers find yourselves drawn to this desolate village by a sense of unease. The eerie silence is broken only by the distant howls of wolves and the dripping of rain from rotting buildings. The villagers speak of mysterious disappearances, and rumors swirl of curses, ancient evils, and long-forgotten tragedies buried beneath the town's history.

Sister Agnes, the village's abbess, has lost several souls under her care, and she begs you to help uncover the truth. Will you delve into Hollowbrook's dark past and face the horrors that lurk within?
