The air around Dorian grows colder, the mist thicker, and he can feel an unseen presence watching him, probing his thoughts, searching for something. He shivers, pulling the coat tighter around himself as a gust of wind rips through the campsite. The others have moved away from the possessed Father Vane, who lies convulsing on the ground, still gripped by the malevolent force that possesses him.

Dorian's heart races as he studies the strange markings. They appear to be a series of runes, but they are unlike any he has encountered before. He reaches for his notebook, flipping through pages filled with detailed notes and sketches of various cases, until he finds an empty page. Carefully, he transcribes the runes, his hands trembling as he writes.

As he finishes, a sudden, deafening scream echoes across the marshlands, piercing the night air and causing everyone to jump. The possessed Father Vane's body jerks violently, his eyes rolling back into his head. Dorian feels a chill run down his spine as the entity controlling him seems to sense what he has done.

With a final, violent convulsion, the possessed man falls silent, and Dorian can feel the malevolent presence retreating. He quickly rolls up the parchment, tucking it into his notebook along with his transcription of the runes. As he does so, he feels a sense of unease, as if something is not quite right. But he pushes that feeling aside, knowing that he must learn more about these runes and their connection to the darkness that has taken hold of Ravenmoor.

The night grows colder, the mist thicker, and Dorian can feel a growing sense of dread settling over the village. He knows that they have only scratched the surface of this mystery, and he fears what other horrors lie hidden within the depths of the Aldwick mines. But he also knows that he cannot turn away now - not when so many lives are at stake.

With a heavy heart, Dorian rejoins his companions, sharing with them the strange runes he has discovered. Together, they will face whatever darkness awaits them in the depths of Ravenmoor's mines, determined to uncover the truth and banish the malevolent force that threatens their village.
