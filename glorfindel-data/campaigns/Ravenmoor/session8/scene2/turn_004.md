In the somber depths of Aldwick Mine, the air grows colder and heavier as Sister Isolde Carrow kneels by the water's edge. Her fingers sink into the chilled liquid, and a shiver runs through her, not from the cold but from an uncanny sensation that pervades the darkness. The water seems to swallow her touch, as if it were a part of some vast, unseen entity lurking beneath the surface.

The silence is broken by a distant groan, echoing ominously off the damp stone walls. Isolde's heart races as she stands up, clutching her holy symbol tightly. The mine responds in kind, the air around her shivering with an otherworldly presence that seems to grow more insistent by the moment.

Suddenly, a spectral apparition materializes before them—a twisted figure shrouded in darkness, its features indistinct and malevolent. It looms over Isolde, who cannot help but feel a deep sense of dread as it regards her with soulless eyes. The entity seems to recognize her presence, its energy pulsating menacingly.

Isolde's heart pounds in her chest as she struggles to gather her wits and her faith. She can feel the malevolent force around her, threatening to overwhelm her very being. She takes a deep breath, her hands trembling as she clutches her holy symbol. With a voice that barely carries above the oppressive silence, she begins to chant an ancient prayer of exorcism.

The darkness around them seems to grow denser and more palpable, as if the very air itself were being twisted by the entity's power. Isolde's voice wavers, but she presses on, her resolve unyielding in the face of such overwhelming evil. She can feel her magic flowing through her, filling her with a warmth and strength that she has not felt in years.

The entity recoils at the sound of her voice, its twisted form writhing and contorting as it tries to resist her power. The air around them crackles with electricity, and a brilliant light erupts from Isolde's holy symbol, illuminating the mine in a blinding flash.

As the light fades, the entity is gone—vanquished by Isolde's unyielding faith. But as she catches her breath, she knows that this is not the end of their troubles. The darkness that dwells within Ravenmoor still lurks, waiting for its next victim. They must be vigilant, and they must be ready to face whatever horrors may come next.
