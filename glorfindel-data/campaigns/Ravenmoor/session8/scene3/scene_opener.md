# Scene 3 Opener — The Crack in the Door

## Previously

Vera spoke the forbidden word toward the voice's origin. Emmett traced impossible geometry in St. Aldric's crypt and felt living heat beneath the cold stone. Dorian transcribed the ancient incantation and grew increasingly uneasy. Sister Isolde performed an exorcism in Aldwick Mine that drove a spectral entity back in a blinding flash of divine power.

## Now

The flooding that sealed the mine eight months ago has partially drained. The party stands in the lowest accessible chamber — the antechamber to the sealed door. The iron doors are here. They are warm to the touch. Not warm like stone that has absorbed heat. Warm like something on the other side is breathing.

Father Vane's runes glow faintly in a colour that does not have a name. Not red. Not amber. Something that the eye keeps trying to classify and failing.

There is a crack in the door.

It runs from floor to ceiling on the left panel, perhaps two fingers wide. It was not there when Father Vane sealed the doors. It was not there last week when the first party member passed through this chamber. It is there now.

The sound that comes through the crack is not a sound. It is the absence of sound shaped like a sound. The party's ears keep hearing something that their brains will not process.

The air near the crack is wrong. Not cold. Not warm. The air near the crack is the same temperature as the inside of a thought.

The Thing has not noticed them.

Yet.

## What the Party Knows

- The runes on the door spell "Awakening" across three languages. The third language is unknown.
- The flooding sealed it. The flooding is receding.
- Father Vane performed the rite that holds the door. It cost him sleep. He has not slept properly since.
- Vera's grandmother told her one thing about what lies below mines like this: *Do not let it learn your shape.*
- The crack is new. Something pushed from the inside.
