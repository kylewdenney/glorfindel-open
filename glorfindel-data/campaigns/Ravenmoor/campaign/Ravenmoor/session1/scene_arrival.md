# Session 1 — Arrival in Ravenmoor: A Gothic Horror Tale

The rain pelted against the party as they disembarked from their carriage at the Crossed Road junction, a desolate and eerie place that seemed to drip with a foreboding energy. The relentless downpour had painted the iron cross that marked the junction an ominous shade of rust, and the number "11 DAYS" was etched into the signpost with a desperate urgency.

**Sister Isolde Carrow**, her hood pulled low over her face to shield herself from the elements, felt a shiver run down her spine as she gazed upon the village of Ravenmoor. She had received a letter postmarked from this very place three weeks ago, the cryptic message demanding their immediate presence before the new moon. Her divine magic still worked, but she feared it more than ever since losing her faith and being unable to save a dying child.

**Dorian Ashgrove**, his leather notebook clutched tightly in his hands, muttered to himself as he scrutinized the village with an investigator's keen eye. His paranoia and brilliant mind had led him down a dangerous path before, but he couldn't shake the feeling that something here was amiss—something more than just the incessant rain.

**Emmett Grave**, the self-proclaimed archaeologist who wore death like a second skin, seemed unusually cheerful as he surveyed the decaying buildings. His knowledge of burial customs and undead types proved valuable as they traversed the foggy streets, his immunity to fear serving him well amidst the chilling atmosphere.

**Vera Nighthollow**, the enigmatic witch who conversed with animals and lived on the fringes of society, sensed a dark presence lurking in the village. She felt it creeping through the marshy air, whispering tales of despair and torment to those foolish enough to listen.

As they approached the Aldwick Inn, the party could see flickering lanterns casting eerie shadows upon the weather-beaten facade. A cold wind blew in from the marshes, carrying with it a chill that seemed to seep into their very souls. They knew they had arrived at the heart of darkness.

The roll of a d20 revealed a result of 9. This number represents the party's Fear Save against the oppressive atmosphere of Ravenmoor, which tested their resolve as they ventured further into the village's chilling embrace.
