# Session 1 - Scene 2: The Unsettling Debate of Ravenmoor's Fated Five
As the clock struck midnight, the once lively tavern of Hollowbrook's Embrace fell silent, save for the distant howls of wolves and the dripping rain from decaying buildings. The flickering candles cast eerie shadows that danced menacingly on the walls, painting an unsettling tableau as the party gathered around a worn oak table to debate their course of action.

Sister Isolde Carrow, her silver holy symbol clenched tightly in her hand, advocated for venturing to the well to the east. Her voice wavered slightly as she spoke, betraying the heavy weight of responsibility she felt for each life lost. She argued that their faith demanded they uncover the secrets that had led to this tragedy.

Dorian Ashgrove, a man whose disgrace had followed him like a dark cloud, supported Sister Isolde's argument but with a hint of caution in his voice. He suggested that they should wait until dawn, when the light would banish some of the darkness that seemed to cling to this forsaken village.

Emmett Grave, ever cheerful and morally ambiguous, agreed with both of them. With his keen intellect and insatiable curiosity, he saw the potential for historical significance in their investigation, a prize worth braving the unknown.

Vera Nighthollow, a woman who walked the line between good and evil, listened intently as her companions spoke. She sensed spectral figures lurking in the corners of the room, a chilling reminder that they were not alone in their pursuit of truth.

As the debate continued, the dice rolled revealed the party's varying levels of success in their investigation checks (DC 20). Sister Isolde rolled a 16, securing her position as a leader. Dorian Ashgrove, burdened by his disgrace and the fear it instilled, rolled an 8, gaining a Dread Point and causing him to cower for a moment before regaining his composure.

Emmett Grave, ever eager for adventure, rolled a 15, eager to uncover the secrets of Ravenmoor that had long been buried beneath its surface. Vera Nighthollow, with her keen intuition, rolled a 19, sensing the supernatural forces at play and steeled herself for the challenges that lay ahead.

As the discussion came to a close, the party agreed to investigate the eastern well at dawn, with Sister Isolde leading the way. The air was thick with tension as they prepared to face whatever horrors Ravenmoor had in store for them.
