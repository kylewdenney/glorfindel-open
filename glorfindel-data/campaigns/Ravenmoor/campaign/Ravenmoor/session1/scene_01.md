# Session 1: Arrival in Ravenmoor - The Common Room of the Aldwick Inn

As the moon hung low and heavy behind a veil of storm-laden clouds, the party — Sister Isolde Carrow, Dorian Ashgrove, Emmett Grave, and Vera Nighthollow — sought refuge from the relentless rain at the Aldwick Inn. The innkeeper, Marta Voss, greeted them with a stoic nod before retreating into the shadows of her establishment. A sense of unease permeated the air as they made their way through the dimly lit common room, their footsteps echoing off the ancient stone walls.

Four patrons occupied the space, each huddled over their drinks. The party's eyes were drawn to Old Tomas, the gravedigger, whose worn and weathered face bore the weight of countless burials. Iron nails clutched in his gnarled fingers seemed to exude an eerie aura as they jingled softly against one another.

"What brings you to our humble village?" Old Tomas asked, his voice a gravelly whisper that seemed to shake the very walls of the inn.

Sister Isolde approached him, her resolve unwavering. She knew that if they were to solve this mystery and save Ravenmoor from its dark secrets, it would require persistence and courage. With a steady hand, she reached out and clasped Old Tomas' calloused fingers. "We seek information about the well, the mill, and the marsh girl," she said, her voice barely audible above the distant howls of wolves.

Old Tomas stared intently into her eyes, his gaze piercing through the gloom. After a moment's hesitation, he leaned in closer and spoke in hushed tones: "I warn you not to disturb the forty-three... Some of 'em asked for it. Some of 'em got it."

The party stood frozen in silence, their minds racing as they pondered Old Tomas' cryptic warning.
