# Session 1 - Scene 3: The Haunting of the Aldwick Inn's Restless Shadows
As morning dawned, the sun struggled to penetrate Ravenmoor's oppressive veil of gloom. The party woke to the lingering chill of dread within the Aldwick Inn, the weight of their restless slumber still heavy upon them. 

A thick mist cloaked the village, as if attempting to conceal its dark secrets further. The inn's once warm and inviting atmosphere now felt cold and foreboding, the flickering candles casting long, twisted shadows that danced menacingly on the walls. 

Sister Isolde Carrow stirred first, clutching her silver holy symbol tightly as she sat up in her bed. Her eyes widened as she glanced around the room, her senses heightened by a newfound fear. She could sense an unsettling presence nearby, a specter of darkness that seemed to be watching them all. 

Dorian Ashgrove stirred next, his brow furrowed with concern as he rubbed the sleep from his eyes. His disgraced past and the night's unsettling events had left him on edge, his mind racing with thoughts of the unknown horrors that lurked within Ravenmoor. 

Emmett Grave stretched out in his bed, a wide grin spreading across his face as he awakened. His curiosity and eagerness for adventure were undiminished, even in the face of such unsettling events. He swung his legs over the side of the bed and stood up, ready to face whatever Ravenmoor had in store for them. 

Vera Nighthollow stirred last, her eyes flickering open as she felt the weight of the spectral presence still lingering near. She let out a low growl, sensing that something was not right within the inn. Her witch's instincts told her to be cautious, to trust her senses and rely on her arcane abilities to protect herself and her companions. 

The party gathered around the table once more, each member of the fated five feeling the weight of their Dread Points heavy upon them. Sister Isolde spoke up first, her voice trembling slightly as she voiced her concerns about the spectral presence they had all felt. Dorian nodded in agreement, his own fear evident in his eyes. Emmett, ever eager for adventure, seemed unfazed by the events of the night before. Vera, her senses attuned to the supernatural, offered words of caution and reassurance. 

As they discussed their plan for the day, the dice rolled revealed their varying levels of success in their social interactions (DC 15). Sister Isolde rolled a 12, her fear still evident despite her best efforts to hide it. Dorian Ashgrove rolled a 16, managing to keep his composure and present a more confident front. Emmett Grave rolled an 8, his enthusiasm getting the better of him at times. Vera Nighthollow, ever attuned to the supernatural, rolled a 20, her words having a powerful impact on the others. 

With their plan set, the party prepared to face whatever Ravenmoor had in store for them, each member carrying the burden of their Dread Points and the weight of the spectral presence that still lingered within the inn.
