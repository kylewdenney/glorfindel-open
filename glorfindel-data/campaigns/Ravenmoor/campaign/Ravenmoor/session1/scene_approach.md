# Scene: Approach to Ravenmoor

As the party treads through the Ashfen Marshes, a foreboding sense of dread envelopes them. The air is thick with the rank odor of the swamp, and the path ahead is a flooded, muddy expanse that squelches underfoot. The distant groan of the mill wheel echoes ominously through the desolate landscape, punctuated by the occasional cawing of ravenous birds lurking in the shadows.

Sister Isolde Carrow, her hood pulled low over her face to shield herself from the elements, feels a shiver run down her spine as she gazes upon the village of Ravenmoor. The relentless rain drenches her cloak and chills her to the bone.

Dorian Ashgrove, his leather notebook clutched tightly in his hands, mutters to himself as he scrutinizes the village with an investigator's keen eye. His paranoia and brilliant mind have led him down a dangerous path before, but he can't shake the feeling that something here is amiss—something more than just the incessant rain.

Emmett Grave, the self-proclaimed archaeologist who wears death like a second skin, seems unusually cheerful as he surveys the decaying buildings. His knowledge of burial customs and undead types prove valuable as they traverse the foggy streets, his immunity to fear serving him well amidst the chilling atmosphere.

Vera Nighthollow, the enigmatic witch who converses with animals and lives on the fringes of society, senses a dark presence lurking in the village. She feels it creeping through the marshy air, whispering tales of despair and torment to those foolish enough to listen.

As they approach the Crossed Road junction, a DC 14 Investigation check by Dorian reveals a cryptic message etched into the signpost: '11 DAYS'. The party presses onward, their footsteps echoing as they enter Ravenmoor proper. They soon reach the Aldwick Inn, where a cold wind blows in from the marshes, carrying with it a foreboding chill.

Upon entering the inn, they encounter Marta Voss, who charges them exorbitant prices for accommodation and asks little in return. The party settles in for the night, ready to uncover the dark secrets that lie hidden within Ravenmoor come morning.
