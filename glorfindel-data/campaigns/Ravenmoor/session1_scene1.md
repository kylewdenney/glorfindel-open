As the moon cast a feeble light upon the desolate landscape, Sister Isolde Carrow, Dorian Ashgrove, Emmett Grave, and Vera Nighthollow ventured deeper into the heart of Ravenmoor. Their destination: the sinister Eastern Well nestled within the Ashfen Marshes. The air was thick with an oppressive silence, punctuated only by the distant howls of wolves and the dripping of rain from decaying buildings. A sense of dread hung heavy over them as they approached the well's ominous stone structure. With a determined stride, they descended into its dark depths. The flickering torchlight revealed an old stone at the base of the well, the remnants of a previous investigation perhaps. Isolde's practiced gaze fell upon it, a 14 on her Investigation check uncovering a surface clue. Meanwhile, Dorian and Vera collaborated to delve deeper, their combined skills revealing a hidden clue — an iron ring with a DC of 25. A shiver ran down Emmett's spine as he failed his Fear Save (DC 16), the cold damp air now heavy with a palpable sense of dread. Despite the lack of a new rope, they pressed on in their exploration, determined to uncover the secrets buried beneath Ravenmoor.
