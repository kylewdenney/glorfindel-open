In the somber hours before dawn, the Aldwick Inn of Ravenmoor served as a gathering place for the intrepid band of adventurers - Dorian Ashgrove, Emmett Grave, Sister Isolde Carrow, and Vera Nighthollow. An icy wind whispered through the cracks of the inn's decaying walls, carrying with it an air of foreboding. The arrival of Old Tomas, Ravenmoor's gravedigger, stirred apprehension as he bore news that would send chills down Dorian's spine - the eastern well, long a source of life for the village, had run dry for the first time in memory. This revelation echoed Dorian's recent nightmare about the mine flood, prompting an Insight check that mirrored Old Tomas' past experience. With a collective sense of unease, the party decided to venture into the mine, leaving behind their temporary refuge as they braved the darkness that enshrouded Ravenmoor once more.

As day turned to night, the adventurers ascended the east tower, each step a defiant challenge against the encroaching decay of the village. Father Vane warned them not to answer whatever might ask them, fearing it would recognize their voices. Upon reaching the top, they found Casimir Voss' incantations ceasing as an oppressive silence descended upon them. The party stood before the unknown, their hearts pounding in unison with the silence that enveloped them, while Ravenmoor watched ominously from afar.

In the moonless night, under the oppressive sky, Sister Isolde led the party towards the Ashfen Marsh mines. Father Vane defied a warning, picking up an iron nail that crumbled in his hand, echoing ominously. The cold air grew colder as they approached, and Vera sensed something watching from the shadows. Isolde spoke resolutely: "We must go in." With heavy hearts and trembling hands, they stepped into the mine, entering Ravenmoor's tragic past and unknown horrors.

In the mine shaft, at thirty feet, Isolde encountered an abandoned miner's boot, sending a chill down her spine. Sixty feet below ground, Dorian found the foreman's work station with unsettling records about a sealed chamber and the foreman's last entry. At ninety feet, they discovered the foreman's coffee cup and logbook, prompting Emmett to prepare for a critical decision as an ominous sound emanated from below, forty feet deeper in the pre-existing chamber.

The party, led by Sister Isolde Carrow, Dorian Ashgrove, Emmett Grave, and Vera Nighthollow, ventured into a timeless chamber in Ravenmoor's Ashfen Marsh. They encountered an ancient being submerged in the center, its gaze fixed upon them. Dorian documented their findings while Emmett attempted to discern its nature through arcane means. Vera sensed a connection between her and the entity, revealing it as older than the village, marsh, or even language itself. The chamber's air grew colder with Father Vane's kneeling before the enigmatic presence.

As the village was engulfed by rising waters from the mine, Vera suggested they answer an entity's question, acknowledging the potential risks. With determination, the party stepped forward, bracing for what lay ahead as they approached the darkness, feeling a chilling reminder of the fragile balance between sanity and madness. The session ended with the party poised at the edge of danger, their fates hanging in the balance, as they stood on the precipice of Ravenmoor's past, present, and future.

The hook into the next session finds the party at the well to the east, under the cover of darkness, waiting for dawn to venture forth and confront whatever lies within. The village watches with bated breath, unsure if their heroes will return or if they themselves are doomed to join the ranks of Ravenmoor's tragic past.
