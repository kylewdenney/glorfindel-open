As the party ascends the east tower, each step is a testament to their resolve as they are drawn deeper into the heart of Ravenmoor's darkness. The air grows colder, heavier with the stench of decay and the whispers of the marsh. The incantations of Casimir grow louder, his spectral voice echoing through the ancient stones like a haunting dirge.

Sister Isolde feels the weight of her fallen cleric status press down upon her shoulders, a heavy burden she carries with every step. Dorian Ashgrove, the disgraced investigator, clings to his faith in reason and logic, but even he cannot ignore the palpable dread that seeps into the very fabric of this cursed village.

Emmett Grave, the graverobber, feels a strange sense of familiarity with the tower's damp walls, as if they speak of secrets long buried in the marsh. He glances at Vera Nighthollow, the witch, who seems to be in her element, her eyes gleaming with an otherworldly light.

Father Vane stands at the landing below the top, his face etched with fear and determination. His voice barely audible, he warns them: "Whatever it asks you — do not answer. If you answer even once, it knows your voice." The gravity of his words hangs in the air like a shroud, adding to the growing sense of dread that surrounds them.

The party reaches the top, the door ajar before them. Casimir's voice suddenly ceases, and an oppressive silence descends upon them. It is as if the very village holds its breath in anticipation of what lies beyond the door.

Standing on the precipice of their greatest fear, the party members feel their hearts pounding in unison with the silence that surrounds them. The ghostly village of Ravenmoor watches from afar, its eerie presence a constant reminder of the horrors they are about to face. Isolde, Dorian, Emmett, and Vera stand united, bound together by their shared dread and determination to confront the darkness that threatens Ravenmoor.
