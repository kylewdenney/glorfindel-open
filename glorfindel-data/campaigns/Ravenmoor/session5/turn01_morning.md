As dawn's first light crept over the Ashfen Marshes, a hush fell upon Ravenmoor like a shroud. The air was thick with an unspoken dread that seemed to whisper through the cobblestone streets and creaking timbers of the Aldwick Inn.

The party sat in silence around the table, each lost in their own thoughts as they stared at the sealed letter from Casimir Voss that lay before them. The ink was still wet on the parchment, a testament to the haste with which it had been delivered by the stable boy who appeared to have run the entire journey.

In the doorway, Old Tomas stood, his gaunt frame and iron-studded bucket casting long shadows across the uneven floorboards. His eyes met Dorian's for a moment before he spoke, his voice barely more than a whisper. "The eastern well ran dry this morning. First time in living memory."

Dorian felt a chill run down his spine as he looked at the gravedigger. Something about Old Tomas' demeanor suggested that he was not simply reporting an event—he was sharing a warning. Dorian took a deep breath, focusing on the familiar weight of his investigator's tools in his pocket, and made his Insight check.

The dice rolled across the table, coming to rest with an ominous thud. As Dorian added his Wisdom modifier, he felt a wave of unease wash over him. With a heavy heart, he announced the result: 8. Old Tomas had seen this before—one week prior to the mine flood.

The party exchanged glances, their faces reflecting a shared understanding: they had to go to the mine. The darkness that haunted Ravenmoor was calling them once again, and they could not ignore its siren song. As they prepared to leave, each took one final look around the Aldwick Inn—a sanctuary that now seemed all too fragile against the encroaching malevolence of their village.
