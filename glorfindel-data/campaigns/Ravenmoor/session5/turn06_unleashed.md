As the party stands at the mine entrance, bathed in the grey daylight, a sense of dread envelops them like a shroud. The sound of water, once distant and ominous, now grows louder, a relentless symphony of doom that echoes through the Ashfen Marshes. The village of Ravenmoor looms ahead, a mere mile away but already under the darkening grip of the rising waters.

Sister Isolde Carrow's heart pounds in her chest as she looks at her companions. The fallen cleric, once a beacon of hope and faith, now faces an enemy that challenges everything she has ever known. Her eyes meet Dorian Ashgrove's, the disgraced investigator whose own secrets and failures have led him to this moment. Emmett Grave, graverobber and archaeologist, stands stoic, his knowledge of the ancient world offering little solace in the face of this unknown terror. Vera Nighthollow, witch, gazes into the distance, her arcana wisdom providing a glimmer of hope amidst the impending disaster.

The air is heavy with tension as they stand at the precipice, the line between life and death blurred by the encroaching waters. Dorian breaks the silence, his voice barely audible above the cacophony of the rising tide. "What do we do?" he asks, not a question but a plea for guidance in this moment of despair.

Vera's eyes narrow as she looks at her companions, her mind racing with possibilities. She knows that to answer the entity's question might bring understanding, but also danger and potential madness. Yet, she also knows that silence may lead to the destruction of Ravenmoor and all its inhabitants. With a determined nod, she responds, "We answer it."

The party steps forward, each taking a deep breath as they prepare to face the unknown. The fate of Ravenmoor hangs in the balance, and they are its only hope. As they move towards the entity that stirs within the darkness, they cannot help but feel a chill run down their spines, a reminder that the line between sanity and madness is thin, and the world of Ravenmoor is quickly slipping into the abyss.
