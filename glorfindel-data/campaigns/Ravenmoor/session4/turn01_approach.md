As the party approached Voss Manor, their footsteps echoed eerily against the damp earth, each step sinking slightly into the ground as if the very land beneath them was yielding to the oppressive atmosphere that surrounded Ravenmoor. The iron gates stood ajar, an ominous invitation that sent shivers down their spines. The sun's feeble rays struggled against the encroaching grey, casting elongated shadows that danced and twisted like spectral apparitions.

Isolde gripped her holy symbol tightly, her eyes fixed on the east tower as a faint glow emanated from within, like a beacon of darkness amidst the gloom. Dorian's keen senses were on high alert, his gaze scanning the grounds for any signs of unnatural activity. Emmett, ever the pragmatist, kept a steady pace, his eyes scanning the manor for any potential loot or clues that might aid their investigation. Vera, however, felt an unsettling presence growing within her, as if the very air was thick with malevolence and the whispers of the dead.

Father Vane, walking alongside them, his face a mask of somber determination, spoke softly of Casimir's fate, warning that answering the entity's questions may not be madness but the beginning of a far worse torment. His words hung heavy in the air, the weight of them pressing down on each member of the party like the crushing embrace of the marsh itself.

As they drew closer to the manor, the ground near the east tower foundation was wet, not rain-wet, but seep-wet – a chilling indication that the water table had risen. The sight sent a shiver down each of their spines, as if the very earth was crying out in response to the darkness that lay within Voss Manor's walls. The party treaded cautiously, their senses heightened, their hearts pounding in their chests as they neared the manor, feeling the growing dread that threatened to consume them whole.
