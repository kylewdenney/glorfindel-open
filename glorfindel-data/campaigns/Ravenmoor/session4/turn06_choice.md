Marta, the proprietress, greeted them with glasses of amber liquid, her eyes probing each face for answers they chose not to give. With a nod and a soft, "Did you answer it?" she set their drinks before them, her silent understanding echoing through the dimly lit common room.

Dorian, his mind still reeling from the encounter, sank into his chair, his gin untouched. The weight of their decision weighed heavily on him, and he couldn't help but feel that they had only delayed the inevitable. As the room spun around him, he wondered if they would ever truly be free of the malevolent entity that now haunted their village.

Emmett, his ever-cheerful demeanor unchanged despite the night's events, raised his glass to Marta in a toast. He knew that their work was far from over, but for now, he would take solace in the warmth of the ale and the camaraderie of his fellow adventurers.

Vera, her Arcana still ringing in her ears from the entity's haunting questions, stared into the depths of her drink. She knew that the ancient force was far from defeated, and she couldn't shake the feeling that it would always find another vessel to torment Ravenmoor.
