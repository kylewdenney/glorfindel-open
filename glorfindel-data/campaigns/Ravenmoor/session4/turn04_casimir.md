As the party steps into Casimir's study, the air is heavy with a palpable sense of dread that clings to their clothing like a foggy mist. The room, once filled with scholarly papers and books, now stands barren and devoid of life, save for the emaciated figure of Casimir Voss. His eyes, sunken and hollow, lock onto the intruders as they enter, his gaze unwavering.

Isolde approaches cautiously, her heart pounding in her chest. She cannot help but feel a deep sense of sorrow for this tortured soul who has been locked in a battle against an unseen enemy for three weeks. As she draws closer, Casimir speaks without preamble, his voice trembling with a desperate urgency that chills the air around them.

"It's still in there," he murmurs, gesturing vaguely towards the window that looks out onto the moonlit village of Ravenmoor. "I can hear it right now. It never stops." His eyes plead with Isolde to understand, to help him find an answer to a question that has consumed him for far too long.

The room falls silent as Dorian steps forward, his investigator's instincts kicking into high gear. He studies Casimir's gaunt form, his mind racing to decipher the meaning behind the man's words. Emmett and Vera stand quietly in the background, their eyes scanning the room for any clue that might offer insight into this mystery.

Just as the tension threatens to become unbearable, Father Vane appears in the doorway, his gaunt face a mask of sorrow. He looks at Casimir for a moment before turning to the party and simply stating, "I'm sorry, boy." With those words, he disappears as mysteriously as he had arrived, leaving the party with more questions than answers.

Isolde watches as Father Vane vanishes, her heart heavy with a growing sense of foreboding. She knows that they have stumbled upon something far greater and darker than any of them could have imagined, and she cannot help but wonder if their arrival in Ravenmoor has sealed their fate.
