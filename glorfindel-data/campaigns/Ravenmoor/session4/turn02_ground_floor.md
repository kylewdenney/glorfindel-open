In the hallowed silence of the moonlit night, the spectral shadows of Ravenmoor seemed to dance more menacingly than ever before as the party approached Voss Manor. The crumbling mansion stood like a grim sentinel over the Ashfen Marshes, its once grand facade now marred by decay and neglect.

As they crossed the threshold into the study, the air grew heavy with an oppressive stillness that seemed to seep from every pore of the ancient stone walls. The flickering candlelight cast a grotesque tableau upon the scene before them - every surface covered in Casimir Voss's frantic handwriting, growing smaller and more desperate as time progressed.

Dorian Ashgrove, his heart pounding with both anticipation and dread, carefully scrutinized the notes, his eyes scanning over scholarly discussions of an entity that was pre-human and non-malevolent, but had begun asking impossible questions about memory, names, and the moment before Casimir's father died. The investigator couldn't shake off a growing sense of unease, as if he were staring into the abyss itself.

Emmett Grave, ever eager to uncover the secrets that lay hidden in Ravenmoor's dark corners, moved methodically through the room, his eyes gleaming with curiosity and excitement. As he studied the notes, however, he couldn't shake off a creeping sense of unease, as if he were being watched by unseen eyes.

Vera Nighthollow, her senses attuned to the supernatural, closed her eyes and reached out with her magic, feeling the residual power in the room that seemed stronger than any she had encountered before. With a shiver, she realized that it wasn't brought here by Casimir - it followed him. The witch couldn't help but feel a growing sense of dread as the realization sunk in.

Sister Isolde Carrow, her silver holy symbol clenched tightly in her hand, stood silently in the center of the room, her gaze fixed upon the countless pages that repeated the phrase 'I don't know.' The fallen cleric couldn't help but feel a growing sense of fear as she listened to the low rhythmic sound coming from above, like someone reading aloud to an empty room.
