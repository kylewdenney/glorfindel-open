# Hook: The Echoing Chant of Ravensong's Whispers
As the rainy mist clings to their clothing and drips from decaying rooftops, the party finds themselves drawn into a somber assembly around the dying embers of a campfire in Ravenmoor. Emmett Grave, with his hands trembling, unfurls an ancient, leather-bound tome he discovered within the abbey ruins.

The ink within the tome appears to be freshly spilled, the parchment stained with the darkness of secrets long hidden and forgotten. As Dorian Ashgrove peeks over Emmett's shoulder, a sinister voice resonates through the night, 'Welcome, seekers of knowledge. Your fate is entwined with Ravenmoor's.'

The party huddles closer, their hearts pounding with anticipation and dread, as Vera Nighthollow senses a malign presence lurking nearby. Sister Isolde Carrow, her eyes filled with determination and fear, whispers, 'This is not the end of our journey, but merely the beginning.'

As the echoes of ravensong fill the air, the party's suspicions of each other continue to grow as they delve deeper into the mysteries of Ravenmoor. The darkness seems to close in around them, and it is unclear whether their fates will lead to salvation or damnation.
