# Key NPCs

## Morgan le Fay
Sovereign of Avalon. Ageless, precise, amused by almost everything. She has been waiting since Arthur crossed the water and has not found the waiting onerous — time moves differently in Avalon, and there is always something interesting happening on the mortal side of the Veil.

She is not the villain of this story. She is also not an ally. She is a sovereign with her own objectives, and her objectives happen to intersect with the party's in some places and conflict in others. She will be honest about the places they conflict. This is not kindness — it is a Fae's precision.

She knew the original knights. She has opinions about their descendants.

---

## Nimue — The Lady of the Lake
She is what Nimue chose to become. The original Nimue made a bargain and it shaped her into something that persists. She is not cruel about it. She is also not the woman she was.

She is the primary keeper of the Veil on the mortal side. She knows every Devotion, every bloodline, every bargain that was ever made at a threshold she maintains. She will help the party if their interests align. She will also let them walk into something terrible if she calculates it necessary for the larger arc.

She remembers the original Lancelot. She has not decided yet what she thinks of Caius.

---

## The Fisher King
He is still there. He has been there since the original wound was dealt and the question was never asked correctly. He exists in a place that is neither Avalon nor Britain — a pavilion beside a river that appears on no map.

He is not suffering dramatically. He has been doing this too long for drama. He is tired. He is patient. He will receive visitors. He will not ask you to heal him — the original bargain requires that *you* ask *him* the question. He cannot prompt it.

The wound is connected to something the party carries. They will not know which one until they are standing in the pavilion.

---

## Cernunnos — Lord of the Hunt
He is not Gawain's patron specifically. He is older than the Round Table. He is interested in the bloodline because the original oath of the Hunt was made to him directly, and he has not released it.

He appears at crossroads, at dusk, at the edge of forests where the old trees start. He has antlers sometimes. He has a man's face always. He finds mortals briefly interesting, the way a hunting hound finds a scent interesting. He will not hurt the party unless they are the quarry.

Whether they become the quarry depends on choices they haven't made yet.

---

## Fionn — A Sídhe Lord of the Old Hills
He is the first Fae they will meet. He has been under the hill at Dun Scaith for longer than the Roman road that runs past it has existed. He is courteous in the old style — elaborate, precise, patient. He will offer hospitality.

He knew the original knights. He has been expecting someone to come. He is not surprised it took this long — Romans are slow to admit what they've inherited.

He has a debt to the line of Kay that predates Sioned's ledger entry. He knows what it says. He has not offered to explain it. He is waiting to be asked correctly.

---

## Branwen's Ghost — The Unfinished Witness
Not literally a ghost. A presence. Branwen of the Fens made a bargain of her own, separate from the Round Table's original contracts, and it left an impression in certain places near water and marshland. The party will encounter it before they understand what it is.

It does not speak. It witnesses. Things it has witnessed have a tendency to matter later.
