# The Party

## Caius ap Llywarch
*Descendant of Lancelot du Lac. Devotion: THE SWORD.*

Third generation Roman-British. His grandfather served in Arthur's court — or so the family says. Caius has known since he was twelve that he is the best fighter anyone around him has ever seen. He doesn't understand why. He doesn't understand why he cannot make himself lose even a practice bout, even when losing would be the gracious thing. He doesn't understand why, when he won a honour contest last spring, he felt nothing but the shape of the next one.

He is not arrogant. He is frustrated. There is a difference.

What he wants: to be good at something that isn't killing.
What he fears: that the Sword is all there is.

## Elen ferch Maelog
*Descendant of Morgana le Fay. Devotion: THE VEIL.*

Her mother's mother's mother was Morgana's daughter, or near enough — the family doesn't say it plainly, and Elen has learned not to ask. She grew up near a lake. Things looked different to her there. The water had a second surface. Animals stopped before she made sound. She thought this was normal until she was old enough to understand that no one else was seeing it.

She came south with a Roman surveying party as their interpreter. She speaks four languages and reads two others. What she is actually doing is trying to understand what she's been seeing her whole life before it gets her killed.

What she wants: to know the name of the thing that has been watching her since childhood.
What she fears: that it already knows hers.

**Devotion: THE VEIL — Rating 1**

## Peredur map Gwrtheyrn
*Descendant of Percival. Devotion: THE GRAIL.*

He asks questions that make people uncomfortable. He has always done this. He asked his father why the old hill fort was abandoned and his father said don't ask. He asked the priest why the old words in the liturgy were different from the Latin he'd been taught and the priest said don't ask. He stopped asking people and started reading everything he could find.

He is earnest in a way that functions as a kind of weapon — you cannot dismiss him because he clearly means it. He doesn't know about his bloodline. He just knows that he cannot stop looking for the thing that is just out of view.

What he wants: to ask the question that no one else has been willing to ask.
What he fears: that he already asked it once and gave the wrong answer.

**Devotion: THE GRAIL — Rating 1**

## Sioned ferch Cai
*Descendant of Kay / Cai ap Cynyr. Devotion: THE TABLE.*

She manages things. She has always managed things. The surveying party that hired her as a logistician got significantly more than they expected — she reorganized their supply chain in the first week, resolved a land dispute that had been running three years, and quietly ensured that two men who were about to knife each other never ended up in the same camp at the same time.

She knows what everyone owes. She cannot stop knowing. She keeps a ledger and some of what's in it she has never shown anyone. There is an entry in there she cannot account for — a debt that predates her family, written in a hand she doesn't recognize, in a language she can almost read.

What she wants: to close the accounts. All of them.
What she fears: finding out what's in the ledger she inherited.

**BLADE +1 | PRESENCE +3 | LORE +2 | RITUAL +1 | OFFERING +3 | VEIL +1**
**Devotion: THE TABLE — Rating 1**
