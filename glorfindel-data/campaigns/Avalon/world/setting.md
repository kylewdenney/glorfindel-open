# Setting — Post-Roman Britain, Late Fifth Century

## The World as It Is

The legions are gone. They left roads, aqueducts, administrative Latin, and a population that built its identity around being Roman and now isn't sure what it is. The infrastructure still functions — barely. The villas are maintained — some of them. The titles persist. The courts persist.

What replaced Roman order is not chaos. It is something older reasserting itself. The hill forts are being reoccupied. The old speech is coming back in places that suppressed it for three generations. The druids are not gone — they went quiet and they are becoming unquiet.

Arthur's court was two generations ago. It is already legend in some mouths and bitter memory in others. The Round Table existed. People who were there are now old or dead. Their children inherited titles and Roman manners and a faint sense that something important happened that no one fully explained.

## The Veil

The boundary between Britain and the Otherworld is thinner than it has been in centuries. This is not a new phenomenon — it has been thinning since Arthur crossed the water to Avalon. Camelot's dissolution weakened the structures that the original knights maintained through their Devotions.

Places where the Veil is thin:
- Bodies of still water, especially at dawn and dusk
- Hill forts built on older foundations
- Standing stone circles
- Crossroads at the solstice
- Any place where something was sacrificed and not properly closed

The locals know. They leave offerings at the old places. They don't walk certain roads after dark. The Roman-descended gentry call this superstition and are careful not to be seen doing it themselves while also doing it quietly.

## The Immediate Context

A Roman administrative party has been surveying land claims in the western territories — disputed ground between three families with overlapping hereditary claims dating back to before the legions left. Standard work. Boring work.

They have been hired to resolve the dispute or at least produce a document that gives some party legal standing. The four player characters are part of this party in different capacities.

The disputed land includes a hill fort called Dun Scaith. The locals will not go inside it. The Roman party doesn't have time for local superstition.

## Campaign Conditions at Open

- **The Veil Is Thin** — Dun Scaith sits on a major threshold. Something has been stirring under that hill.
- **The Bloodlines Are Known** — Fionn has been watching the road for two weeks. He knew they were coming before they did.
- **The Fisher King's Wound Is Open** — something changed recently; the pavilion is reachable for the first time in a generation.
- **No One Has Invoked The Table In Living Memory** — the Devotions are dormant, Rating 1, waiting.
