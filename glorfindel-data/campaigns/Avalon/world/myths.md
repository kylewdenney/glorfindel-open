# The Living Myths

These are not stories. They are contracts that predate the Round Table, that Arthur's court channeled and gave Roman shape to, and that did not expire when Camelot fell. Each myth is active. Each has a bearer — whether or not that bearer knows it.

## The Sword in the Stone
The myth: sovereignty is not inherited. It is proven. The right person pulls; everyone else bleeds.
The living truth: the Sword Devotion does not belong to whoever is strongest. It belongs to whoever the myth has claimed. Caius did not choose this. The myth found his bloodline because his ancestor earned it through something he never wrote down.
Current state: active, restless. The Sword has been waiting for a contest worth having.

## The Holy Grail
The myth: the perfect question asked in the right room heals the land. Percival almost asked it. Almost.
The living truth: the Grail is not a cup. It is the act of asking the unanswerable question without flinching from the answer. The Fisher King's wound stays open because the question has not been asked correctly since Percival's failure.
Current state: urgent. The wound is worse. The land around the pavilion is grey.

## The Wound of the Fisher King
The myth: the king and the land are one. A wounded king means a wounded land. It cannot heal until someone asks why he suffers.
The living truth: the wound is not metaphor. The pavilion exists. The Fisher King exists. He has been waiting since before Arthur. The original wound predates Camelot — it is why Merlin built what he built.
Current state: actively bleeding. Someone in the party carries the thread that connects to it.

## The Wild Hunt
The myth: some things, once pursued, must be brought to account. The Hunt does not stop. The Hunter does not rest.
The living truth: the Gawain line swore an oath to Cernunnos that was never formally released. Every generation produces one child who cannot stop pursuing what they've named. Sometimes this is heroic. Sometimes it is a curse.
Current state: quiet. No Hunt has been declared. Cernunnos is watching the party with the patient interest of something that hasn't been invoked in a while.

## The Veil of Morgana
The myth: some things can only be seen from the other side. The sorceress who was called evil was the one who could see clearly. Seeing clearly is its own cost.
The living truth: Morgana's line carries the ability to perceive the Otherworld's shape overlaid on the mortal world. Elen sees two surfaces on still water. She has always seen this. She thought she was strange. She is accurate.
Current state: active and unacknowledged. The Sídhe have been watching Elen since she was a child. No one told her.

## The Round Table
The myth: the Table made every seat equal. The Code made obligation mutual. What kept Arthur's court from becoming every other court was that the obligations ran both directions — king to knight, knight to king.
The living truth: the Table as structure is gone. The Table as obligation is not. The debts don't vanish because the furniture does. Sioned's ledger has entries she didn't write. She inherited them.
Current state: dormant. The last invocation was two generations ago. Fionn remembers it. He is curious whether the new bearers know what they're holding.
