The melody swirls around him, filling his mind with visions of ancient knights and legendary battles. A surge of power courses through him, making each movement more fluid, each strike sharper.

Morgan le Fay watches from the sidelines, her eyes glinting with amusement and curiosity. Nimue, the Lady of the Lake, is there too, her gaze locked onto Caius. The Fisher King, somewhere between Avalon and Brita, feels a stirring within him, sensing the potential of a new knight.
