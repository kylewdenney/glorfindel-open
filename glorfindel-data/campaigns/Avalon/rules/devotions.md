# Devotions — The Living Contracts

A Devotion is not a choice. It is inherited through blood, active whether the bearer knows it or not. The original knights of Arthur's court made real bargains with the Otherworld. Those bargains did not expire.

## The Six Devotions

### THE SWORD
*Carried by the line of Lancelot*

**The gift**: When you act from strength, precision, or righteous violence, +Devotion to BLADE.
**The demand**: You cannot yield in a contest of honour. Surrender is a break of contract.
**The power** (Devotion 3): In a moment of genuine crisis, call on The Sword — your next BLADE roll cannot fail catastrophically. The DC drops by 6.
**The cost**: The Fae note every victory. Each use of the power adds to what is owed.

---

### THE GRAIL
*Carried by the line of Percival*

**The gift**: When you pursue a question to its true answer, +Devotion to LORE or VEIL.
**The demand**: You cannot accept a partial answer. If a door is open, you must go through it.
**The power** (Devotion 3): Ask the Fisher King's Question — one truth about any person or place in the scene is revealed, whether they wish it or not.
**The cost**: You cannot unknow what you find. The Fisher King's wound deepens each time.

---

### THE WOUND
*Carried by the Fisher King's bloodline — a broken Devotion, inheritance of absence*

**The gift**: When you endure what should break you, +Devotion to any check made to keep going.
**The demand**: You must name what is wrong. Silence about your own wound costs you the gift.
**The power** (Devotion 3): The Wound speaks — in a scene of suffering or stalemate, you may declare that something changes because it has been held long enough. The DM must find the turn.
**The cost**: The power only works when you have already lost something in this session.

---

### THE HUNT
*Carried by the line of Gawain*

**The gift**: When you pursue something you have sworn to find or bring to account, +Devotion to BLADE or PRESENCE.
**The demand**: Once declared, a Hunt cannot be called off. You pursue or you break.
**The power** (Devotion 3): The quarry cannot hide — any attempt to conceal, flee, or misdirect automatically fails against you until the scene ends.
**The cost**: The Fae choose what you find at the end. Not always what you hunted.

---

### THE VEIL
*Carried by the line of Morgana*

**The gift**: When you perceive the Other-shape of a thing, +Devotion to VEIL or RITUAL.
**The demand**: You cannot pretend not to see. What you witness, you must account for.
**The power** (Devotion 3): Step sideways — you perceive the scene as the Fae perceive it. All hidden things, all true shapes, all Fae present become visible for one exchange.
**The cost**: The Fae see you in return. Something notes your name.

---

### THE TABLE
*Carried by the line of Kay*

**The gift**: When you make it possible for another character's Devotion to function, +Devotion to PRESENCE or OFFERING.
**The demand**: You keep the accounts. You know what everyone owes and has paid. You cannot pretend a debt doesn't exist.
**The power** (Devotion 3): Invoke the Table — declare one obligation between any two parties in the scene, including Fae. They must acknowledge it. They do not have to honour it, but they must *acknowledge it*.
**The cost**: Invoking the Table means you are party to the obligation. The Fae count you as a creditor and a debtor both.

---

## Devotion Rating Advancement

Devotion increases when:
- You act in accordance with the demand at genuine personal cost
- A Fae formally recognizes your Devotion (they will not do this lightly)
- You complete the full ritual sequence and the Fae accept your lineage

Devotion decreases when:
- You act directly against your demand in a scene where the Fae are present
- You break a bargain made in your Devotion's name
- You deny your lineage to someone who knows the truth
