# Dice and Abilities — Avalon

## The Six Checks

All checks use **d20 + modifier**. Describe what your character does before rolling. The fiction informs the roll; the roll informs what happens next.

| Check | Use it when… |
|-------|-------------|
| **BLADE** | You fight, move under pressure, or act in a moment of physical crisis |
| **PRESENCE** | You speak where it matters — command, persuasion, holding ground in a room |
| **LORE** | You reach for knowledge of myth, lineage, ritual, or the shape of old things |
| **RITUAL** | You open contact with the Otherworld — structured, intentional, dangerous |
| **OFFERING** | You give something to establish or honour terms with the Fae — what you surrender shapes what you receive |
| **VEIL** | You perceive what is hidden — the Other-shape of a place or person, what the Fae are not saying |

## Difficulty

| DC | Situation |
|----|-----------|
| 10 | Routine. No one is actively working against you. |
| 14 | Contested. Stakes are real or the other party has reason to resist. |
| 18 | Hard. A powerful presence, genuine danger, or a Fae who is testing you. |
| 22 | Dire. The boundary between worlds is in play. You do it or you don't. |
| 26 | Avalon-deep. You are in the full presence of something ancient. Modifiers almost don't matter. |

## Devotion Dice

Each character carries a Devotion — a living mythic contract inherited through blood. When you act *in accordance* with your Devotion, add your **Devotion rating** as a flat bonus to the roll.

| Devotion Rating | Meaning |
|----------------|---------|
| 1 | The bloodline is thin. The old contract is stirring. |
| 2 | The Devotion is awake. The Fae have noticed you. |
| 3 | The Devotion is active. Avalon is close when you act. |

**Acting against your Devotion**: you may do it. You lose the bonus. If you do it in a scene where the Fae are watching, you owe something.

## Fae Ritual Sequence

Fae interaction is not a single roll. It is a three-part sequence:

1. **RITUAL** — Open the contact. Failure means the Fae don't answer, or something else does.
2. **OFFERING** — Establish terms. What you put forward shapes the bargain. A poor offering doesn't fail — it invites counter-terms.
3. **LORE or VEIL** — Understand what is actually being asked. The Fae rarely lie. They are very precise about what they say.

All three results combine: full success on all three is a clean bargain. Partial success introduces a complication. Failure on RITUAL with success on OFFERING means something arrived that you didn't invite.

## Blessings

A blessing is a Fae gift that attaches to a character after a successful ritual sequence. It persists until the debt it represents is paid or the bargain breaks.

Blessings give **+2 to a specific check in specific circumstances** defined by the Fae at the time of giving. They are never unconditional.

## Offerings

Offerings are not optional. They are the grammar of Fae interaction. Common offering types:

| Offering | What it signals |
|----------|----------------|
| **Blood** | Personal cost, lineage, sincerity |
| **Name** | Trust and vulnerability — Fae may use a true name once |
| **Memory** | Something real is surrendered; it is gone |
| **Promise** | Binds future action — the Fae will collect |
| **Service** | Open-ended; the Fae will define the terms when ready |

An OFFERING roll determines whether the offering is *received as intended*. A high roll means the Fae find it sufficient. A low roll means they find it... interesting in a different way.

## Dice Notations
- `d20+mod` — standard check
- `2d20 keep highest` — advantage (preparation, ally support, acting at the heart of your Devotion)
- `2d20 keep lowest` — disadvantage (exhausted, lying to someone who knows the old laws, a broken offering)
- `d6` — minor consequence or partial success detail
