In the fading twilight, the land of Avalon was suffused with a palpable tension as its inhabitants prepared for an impending supernatural event. Caius, the knight, paced restlessly in the dimly lit training yard, clutching THE SWORD, while Elen, the seeress, sensed the thin veil between worlds humming with ancient energy. Peredur, devoted to the Grail, was engrossed in his scriptures, oblivious to the ground's subtle shifting beneath him. The absence of Sioned, the scribe ferch Cai, added to the foreboding atmosphere as whispers of the mysterious Fisher King echoed through the halls of Camelot.

The first signs of the impending crisis arose when an ancient boot print appeared in the training yard, its iron-laced leather resonating with tales of knights and legends. As the players traced its outline, a sudden tremor echoed through the air, darkening the sky. Sensing an unseen presence, they held THE SWORD, feeling its power surge as Nimue, the Lady of the Lake, emerged from the shadows. She spoke cryptically about the perilous journey ahead, the healing of the Fisher King, and the weight of Britain's fate on their shoulders, foreshadowing a path of mystery and adventure.

Elen's senses were further tested when she reached out to the veil, feeling a surge of power as it parted, revealing an alien Other-world. A dark figure stood atop a pillar, eyes locked on hers, causing dread. Elen approached, the figure descended, and the ground trembled beneath her feet. With a cry, she pushed against the Veil, shattering it, allowing the dark figure to enter the training yard.

Meanwhile, Peredur found himself at an ancient shrine's door, experiencing a chilling sensation upon touch. The door creaked open to reveal a dark chamber housing the enigmatic Fisher King. Peredur asked his prepared question, and was told that healing would come when he found one who is wounded but not bleeding, during the time of spring's first blossoms after winter's thaw. As he left, the Fisher King warned him subtly that wounds aren't always visible.

Upon Sioned's return to Camelot, she found Peredur in contemplation before an ancient shrine. Upon reunion, Sioned shared her ledger's deciphered words but at a cost. With widened eyes, Peredur listened and then revealed that the Veil was weakening, and Nimue could no longer hold back the darkness from Avalon. The wind grew colder, a whispering omen from the depths of Avalor, foreboding an impending battle to protect their world.

As the session ended, Sioned and Peredur pondered the future, sensing the approaching malevolence lurking just beyond Camelot's walls. They knew that the path ahead would be fraught with danger, but they were determined to heal the Fisher King and protect Avalon from the encroaching darkness. The hook into the next session was clear: prepare for a battle against the unknown Other-world invader and find the one who is wounded but not bleeding to save the Fisher King.
