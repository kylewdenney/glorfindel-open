In the dim light of the twilight, Caius finds himself standing atop a craggy hill, the wind whipping around him as he clutches the hilt of The Sword, his Devotion, tightly in his calloused hands. He had attempted to create a grounding beacon to guide others to Avalon, but alas, the RITUAL roll revealed a failure.

The surrounding landscape seems to pulse with an unseen energy, a sense of disquiet that The Sword has been detecting for days now. Caius feels it too, a nagging unease that sits heavy in his chest, a weight that threatens to crush him under its shadow. He takes a deep breath and tries to focus, to will himself into connecting with the Veil, but it remains stubbornly elusive.

Suddenly, the ground beneath Caius shakes, sending him stumbling backwards. He recovers his balance just in time to see a gust of wind tear across the landscape, leaving behind it a swirling vortex that seems to suck at the very air around it. The Sword glows brightly in his hands, its blade pulsing with an eerie light.

From the vortex emerges Nimue, The Lady of the Lake. She floats effortlessly towards Caius, her eyes filled with a mixture of amusement and curiosity. "Ah, young Caius ap Llywarch," she says, her voice like the rustling of leaves on a windy day. "I have been expecting you."

Caius feels a cold chill run down his spine as he realizes that Nimue knows him, knows about The Sword and its connection to Avalon. He swallows hard, trying to find his voice. "Why are you here?" he asks, his tone shaky.

Nimue smiles enigmatically. "I have my reasons," she replies cryptically. "But perhaps we can help each other. You wish to establish a grounding beacon to Avalon, and I could certainly aid in that endeavor. In return, I would like to discuss the sensations your sword has been detecting."
