"Your family has been in arrears for far too long," one of them murmurs, its voice a haunting echo that sends shivers down your spine. The other two nod in agreement, their forms flickering like ghostly flames.

With the failed offering, you feel the weight of your words hanging heavily between you and your spectral creditors. They lean closer, a sense of impending menace emanating from them. You can feel the ancient debt you bear growing heavier with each passing moment.

"You have brought us something of value," the first figure intones, gesturing towards the glowing words on the parchment. "But it seems that is not enough to settle what your family owes."

The figures close ranks around you, their cold breaths washing over you like a winter wind. You feel a sudden chill grip your heart as they demand more. More of your family's lands, more secrets, more of yourself. The world seems to close in around you, and the darkness threatens to consume you whole.

But you stand firm, drawing on reserves of strength you never knew you possessed. You look deep into their ghostly eyes and say, "I will give you all I have, but I will not be taken. My family's name has been tarnished enough."

For a moment, the figures seem to pause, as if considering your defiance. Then they laugh—a sound that echoes like the distant tolling of a bell. "Very well," one of them says. "We accept your terms, but remember this: you have bought only a temporary reprieve."

With a swiftness that belies their spectral forms, the figures dissolve into mist, leaving you alone on the shoreline. As dawn breaks and the first light of day reaches Avalon's shores, you find yourself filled with a newfound determination—to right old wrongs, to honor your family's name, and to unravel the tangled threads of this ancient debt.

You step into the coracle and push off from the shore, the misty waters of Avalon lapping at the hull as you set forth on a journey that will test the limits of your courage, your strength, and your very soul.
