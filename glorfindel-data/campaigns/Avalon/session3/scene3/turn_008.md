In the fading light of twilight, you stand before the ethereal figures that have materialized at the water's edge. The glowing ledger, with its cryptic inscription, lies open before them, as if offering a bridge between worlds.

The figures regard you for a moment, their translucent forms casting eerie shadows upon the shore. Their voices, soft whispers carried on the wind, echo your words back to you: "Name your price and pay it."

With a deep breath, you reach into your tunic, pulling out a pouch filled with gold coins. You offer it to them, each coin glinting in the dying light like a star fallen from the heavens. The figures regard the offering for an eternity that feels like a heartbeat and then...

They laugh. Not a human laughter, but a sound that echoes through your very soul, chilling you to the core. One of them steps forward, reaching out to touch the gold with a finger as delicate as moonlight. The pouch seems to shrivel in her ethereal grasp, leaving only a few scattered coins.

"Your price is not coin," she whispers. "The Cernunnos' heartstone lies deep within the heart of an ancient forest. It will not be given to you easily."

You feel a sudden pull, as if the ground beneath your feet has become taut like a bowstring. The figures are drawing you into Avalon, and there is no escape. You must face the challenges that lie ahead, or remain forever bound by this ancient debt.

As the world around you begins to fade, you feel a strange sense of peace. This is not the end, but merely the beginning of your journey through the mythic substrate beneath the Arthur legend. The pull grows stronger, and with one last look at the shores you have left behind, you step into the unknown.
