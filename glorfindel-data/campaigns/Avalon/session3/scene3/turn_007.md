As you approach the ethereal figures, their translucent forms ripple with an unsettling energy that seems to emanate from within the very fabric of Avalon itself. They regard you coldly, their gazes piercing through your mortal soul as if searching for some hidden truth.

You present them with the ledger, its pages glowing softly in the moonlight. There is a long, tense silence before one of the figures steps forward and reaches out to touch the parchment with trembling fingers. They lean close to inspect it, their face a mask of ancient sorrow. After what feels like an eternity, they pull away and exchange whispered words with their companions.

The figure that had examined your ledger steps back, and the three of them turn towards you as one. Their voices are like the wind whispering through the reeds, yet somehow audible to your mortal ears. "You have come far," they say, "but the debt of your ancestor is not so easily paid."

Your heart races as they explain that the price for closing this ancient account requires something more than simple payment. The debt was incurred when Kay or Cai ap Cynyr, your forebear, sought to possess the very secrets of Avalon and stole from the sanctum of Morgan le Fay herself. To atone for this transgression, you must retrieve an artifact imbued with the power of the Fae—the heartstone of a long-extinct creature known as the Cernunnos.

As they speak, you feel a cold presence behind you, and you turn to see the reeds parting to reveal the thing in them, its eyes glowing like embers in the darkness. You know that if it catches you, you will not make it back before dawn.

With the ethereal figures' demands echoing in your mind, you are left with a choice: face the unknown danger of retrieving the Cernunnos' heartstone or risk being swallowed by the shadows of Avalon forever. The decision lies within your hands, but time is running out.
