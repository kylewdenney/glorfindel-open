In the twilight's hush, the ancient stones of Avalon whisper their secrets to those who dare listen. As you approach the water's edge, the three figures — ethereal and ageless— stand like sentinels upon the sands. Their eyes burn with a distant fire, and you feel the weight of unspoken histories in their presence.

You lay your ledger before them, its pages etched with an entry that defies reason. The air around it crackles with energy, as if the very parchment is alive. The figures regard you with a mix of curiosity and anticipation, their eyes never leaving the cryptic debt before them.

The elder of the three speaks in a voice like wind over water, "You have come to Avalon seeking resolution for a debt that has haunted your family for generations. We have waited for one such as you to take up this burden." He reaches out a translucent hand, and the ledger trembles in response.

"Your offering," he continues, "is sufficient to close the account. But there is more at stake here than mere debt repayment. The debt you bear is a tangled knot, woven from threads of blood, honor, and destiny. By paying it, you will find yourself forever entwined with the fate of Avalon."

You steel yourself, ready to meet whatever lies ahead. "I understand the stakes," you say, your voice steady despite the dread that gnaws at your heart. "I am prepared to pay the price for this debt, and I will return to my world before dawn."

The elder smiles, a cold, pale thing that sends a shiver down your spine. "Then let us make our bargain," he says, reaching out to take your hand. As you touch his icy flesh, you feel a sudden flood of visions— glimpses of battles fought and lost, whispered secrets shared in darkened chambers, and the slow decay of empires.

But amidst the darkness, there is also hope, a faint, flickering light that calls to something deep within you. This is your debt, your burden to bear, and it will shape the course of both your worlds. With the elder's touch still on your hand, you feel yourself begin to change—to transform in ways you cannot yet comprehend.

As dawn approaches, you stand upon the shores of Avalon, forever altered by the debt you have paid. The world around you shifts and warps, and as you step back into your own reality, you know that you will never be the same again. Your duty is done, but the true adventure has only just begun.
