In the dim twilight of Avalon's shore, the silhouettes of three ethereal figures emerged from the mists, their forms as ephemeral as whispers carried on the wind. Their eyes glinted with a cold, otherworldly light as they regarded Sioned, her ledger, and the glowing parchment within it.

You approach them with unwavering resolve, your heart pounding like a war drum beneath your breastplate. As you lay the ledger before them, their eyes flicker to the entry that has haunted your family for generations. A hush falls upon the air as if the very earth held its breath in anticipation.

One of the figures steps forward, his form shimmering and shifting like water, and speaks in a voice that echoes through time itself: "The price for closing this debt is high, child of Cai ap Cynyr." He pauses, his gaze never leaving yours, and continues, "You must serve us for one full cycle of the moon. Your devotion to The Table will be tested as you manage our affairs here in Avalon."

Your breath catches in your throat, but you stand tall, your resolve unyielding. "I am willing to pay that price," you say, your voice steady despite the dread gnawing at your insides. "But I must return before dawn."

The figure nods gravely, a ghost of a smile playing on his lips. "As you wish, Sioned ferch Cai. But remember, once you step through that water, you will be bound to us for the duration of your service. Choose wisely."

With those words, the figures dissolve back into the mists, leaving you standing at the edge of the shore, the glowing parchment clutched in your hands, and a decision weighing heavily upon your heart. The sun dips lower in the sky, casting long shadows that dance upon the water's surface. Dawn approaches, and with it, the end of your choice.

As you stand there, contemplating the path before you, the world around you seems to hold its breath, waiting for your decision. The wind whispers through the trees, carrying tales of the old gods and their realm, urging you to choose wisely, for the consequences of this decision will echo through the ages.
