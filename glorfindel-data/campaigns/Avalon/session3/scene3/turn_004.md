In the hallowed twilight of Avalon's shores, the ethereal figures stand as sentinels, their forms flickering like will-o'-wisps in the waning light. The ledger you bear, Sioned ferch Cai, is a beacon to them—a key that unlocks ancient debts and obligations. As you approach, they draw closer, their voices echoing like whispers carried on the wind.

You present the ledger, its pages illuminated by the dying embers of a distant fire. The first figure, an ancient crone with eyes like burning coals, reaches out with gnarled fingers and takes the glowing parchment from your grasp. Her touch sends a shiver down your spine, but you steel yourself against it.

The second figure, a regal man adorned in armor that seems to shift and change with the light, scrutinizes the ledger's contents. His gaze lingers on the impossible entry, his face betraying neither surprise nor anger. The third figure, a child with eyes that seem older than time itself, remains silent, her presence felt more than seen.

You offer your terms: "I know what this debt is. I came here to close the account. Name your price and I will pay it—but I am leaving before dawn." The figures confer amongst themselves, their voices low and laden with an otherworldly cadence that sends a shudder through you.

The crone speaks first: "Your family's debt is great, Sioned ferch Cai. It cannot be paid in one moon cycle or even in several. However, we have agreed to accept your service as the manager of our ethereal affairs for a full moon cycle."

You argue against this fate, but their resolve is unyielding. The child reaches out and takes hold of your wrist, her touch like ice water. "You will serve," she says softly, "and in return, we shall grant you safe passage back to the mortal world before dawn."

As the figures' hold on you tightens, you feel yourself being drawn deeper into Avalon. The landscape around you shifts and warps, the once familiar shores replaced by a dense forest of ancient trees that stretch up towards an unreachable sky. You are bound to them now, Sioned ferch Cai—a servant of the ethereal figures for a full moon cycle.
