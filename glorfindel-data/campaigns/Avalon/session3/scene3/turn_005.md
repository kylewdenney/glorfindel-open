In the twilight hush, the ethereal figures — spectral and ageless — regard you with a mix of curiosity and displeasure. They are as inscrutable as the waters of the pool behind them, their faces shifting like moonlit clouds. You have laid your ledger before them, the impossible entry staring back at you like an ancient prophecy.

One figure steps forward, tendrils of translucent smoke weaving around them. They lean over the parchment, their voice a whisper that echoes in your mind: "Your family has long owed us service, young one. You offer payment? We accept."

You feel the weight of centuries pressing against you as they recount the duties and responsibilities your ancestors have failed to fulfill. The tasks are many, and the time is short. Your heart races, but you remain resolute. You offer what you can — a portion of your own life's work, a share in your family's lands, and any secrets or knowledge that might be valuable to them.

The figures consider your offer for an eternity that feels like a heartbeat. Then, one of them speaks again, their voice softer now: "Your bargain is accepted, Sioned ferch Cai. Serve us well, and we may close this account more swiftly than you expect."

As they speak, the air around you shimmers, and you feel a sudden pull — as if the very fabric of reality is trying to drag you under. You cling to your offerings, your mind focused on the dawn that approaches. With a final surge of will, you step back, breaking free from their grasp.

Behind you, the figures watch you go, their faces unreadable in the gathering gloom. As you walk away, you know that you have bought yourself time, but at what cost? The question hangs heavy on the air as Avalon fades behind you, the water shimmering like a gateway to another world.
