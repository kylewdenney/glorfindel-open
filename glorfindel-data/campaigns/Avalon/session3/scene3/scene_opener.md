## Recap

Sioned ferch Cai did not go to her bed. She found Nimue where Nimue always is when she does not want to be found — at the edge of the standing pool behind the outer chapel, watching the water not move. Sioned showed her the two words. Nimue looked at them for a long time without speaking. Then she said: *you did not write those. Something wrote them through you.* She touched the parchment and the ink shifted — not the letters, but the space between them, which widened like a door.

Sioned stepped through.

She did not mean to.

---

Avalon in the small hours before dawn is not the Avalon of songs. There is no apple-blossom light, no silver towers. There is a grey shore, and water that reflects a sky Sioned does not recognise, and behind her the door has already closed. The parchment is still in her hand. The two words glow faintly — not with warmth, but with the cold certainty of something that knows exactly where it is.

She has until the sky lightens to find her way back. In Avalon, the sky does not lighten on its own.

At the shoreline, half-submerged, is a coracle that should not float. A lantern hangs from its prow, burning with a flame that has no fuel. Three figures stand at the water's edge watching her — not threatening, not welcoming. Waiting.

Somewhere behind her, in the reeds, something is already following.

**Characters:** Sioned ferch Cai

## Recommended Paths

- **Sioned ferch Cai**: You have the parchment, the two words, and your Devotion. The coracle is your only way across — but the three figures at the water's edge will have conditions. You can pay them with an offering, trick them with your Table-born cunning, or you can ignore the coracle entirely and look for a second door. The thing in the reeds is getting closer. Choose fast.
