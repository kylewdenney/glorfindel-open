## Recap

Session 2 ended at the gate of Camelot in the small hours before dawn. Sioned ferch Cai returned from Avalon changed — she can read two more words of the impossible entry in her ledger, though the cost is still unclear. She found Peredur at the old shrine on the edge of town, where he had just spoken with the Fisher King. The King's answer was a riddle: *thou shalt be healed when thou findest one wounded yet not bleeding, in the season when winter loosens and spring blooms.* Peredur carries that riddle now like a stone in his chest. Elen, in the training yard, reached through the Veil and saw a dark figure on a pillar — she pushed against the Veil hard enough to shatter it, and the figure stepped through into the mortal yard. What it was, and where it went, she does not know. Caius found the Fisher King's bootprint in the dirt, then knelt in the dark while Nimue warned him that the right question had not yet been asked, and that all of Britain's fate depends on its answer.

---

It is not yet dawn. The four of them are together now, outside the shrine, at the edge of Camelot's outer yard. The wind has gone still in the way that means something is listening. The sky above is the deep blue before first light, and the stars look different than they should — wrong number, wrong positions, as though the sky has been quietly adjusted. Inside the shrine, the door stands open. No one opened it.

Whatever Elen let through the Veil last night, it is somewhere in Camelot now. Sioned has two new words from a ledger that should not be readable. Peredur has a question that is also an answer. Caius has a bootprint and a sword that hums in his hand like a tuning fork.

**Characters:** Caius ap Llywarch, Elen ferch Maelog, Peredur map Gwrtheyrn, Sioned ferch Cai

## Recommended Paths

- **Caius ap Llywarch**: The shrine door is open — something came out of it, or something was already inside. Your sword is warm. Go in. Find out what the Fisher King left behind, if anything. You have the best blade and the strongest reason not to flinch.
- **Elen ferch Maelog**: What you let through the Veil in the training yard — you felt it before you saw it. You can feel it now, somewhere in Camelot, moving. Use the Veil to track it before it reaches the keep.
- **Peredur map Gwrtheyrn**: The Fisher King said *one wounded yet not bleeding.* You have been awake all night turning this over. You think you know who he means. Before the sun rises and the court stirs, go to that person. Ask your real question.
- **Sioned ferch Cai**: The two new words you read — you wrote them down before you lost them. You need to show someone who might recognize them. Your instinct is Nimue. She knows every bargain and every bloodline. Find her before the court wakes.
