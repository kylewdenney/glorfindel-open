In the dense heart of Broceliande, where ancient oaks whisper secrets to the wind and the verdant underbrush hums with unseen life, Elen ferch Maelog steps cautiously. The Veil between worlds is thin here, the bond between realms delicate as a spider's thread.

With a steady breath, she closes her eyes, focusing on the connection that has guided them since Camelot. In her mind's eye, a swirling vortex of light and shadow forms, pulsating with an energy that thrums within her veins like a heartbeat. This is the mysterious being, her guide, her enigma.

Reaching out tentatively, she touches the vortex, feeling it recoil at her touch. A sudden jolt of pain lances through her, as if she has plunged her hand into freezing water. She gasps, stumbling back a step, clutching her wounded arm. The vortex flares brighter for an instant, then dims once more.

Suddenly, it speaks. Not with words, but with emotions, impressions that dance across the edges of consciousness like flickering shadows. It is cold, distant, ancient beyond comprehension. It communicates not through love or anger, but through a detached curiosity that leaves Elen feeling both humbled and unnerved.

She retreats, drawing back from the Veil, her heart pounding in her chest. The pain in her arm fades as quickly as it came, leaving only a lingering unease. She has glimpsed something vast and primordial, something that transcends understanding or emotion. And somehow, she knows that this is but a glimpse of the true scale of the mystery before them.

The party, drawn by Elen's cries of pain, rushes to her side. They find her leaning against an ancient oak, her eyes wide with wonder and dread. She tells them what she has seen, and they listen, awed and afraid. For they have touched the hand of something older than the world itself, something that holds within it both the promise and the peril of Avalon.
