As you approach the water's edge, the trio of figures — ethereal and enigmatic— regard you with a mixture of curiosity and anticipation. Their eyes, like twin moons against the backdrop of the twilight sky, seem to pierce through the veil of mortal understanding, yet they remain inscrutable.

You lay the glowing parchment before them, its contents illuminating the grey shore for a moment, casting elongated shadows that dance upon their forms. The three figures study the document, their expressions unchanging but a sense of awareness emanates from them, as if they were ancient stones that had witnessed countless eons pass by.

One figure, taller and more imposing than the others, steps forward. Their voice, deep and resonant, echoes across the water, "The price for closing this account is not one of coin or commodity." The other two figures maintain their silence, watching with unwavering interest.

"You must prove your worth," the tall figure continues, "by completing a task that only those of your kind can accomplish. You have until dawn to succeed, or you will remain here indefinitely."

Your heart races as you take in the gravity of their demand. The world around you shifts, the once grey shore now bathed in an eerie silver light. A sense of dread and wonder fills the air, a reminder that you are not in your homeland anymore, but in a realm where the legends walk amongst mortals.

The tall figure hands you an ornate wooden box, within which lies a single stone with a rune etched upon it. "This is the mark of Avalon. You must find and return it to its rightful place before dawn." With that, they retreat back to the water's edge, disappearing as if consumed by the tide.

You stand alone on the shore, the box in your hands, the rune stone glowing faintly in the moonlight. The air is thick with tension, the waters whispering secrets only the Fae can understand. You must find the stone's resting place and return before dawn, or be trapped forever in this strange land.
