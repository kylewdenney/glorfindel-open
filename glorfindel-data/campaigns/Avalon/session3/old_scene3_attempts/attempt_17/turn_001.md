In the heart of Avalon's ethereal twilight, you approach the three figures that await your arrival at the grey shore. Their faces remain obscured by the mist, but their presence is palpable, a somber symphony of ancient power and timeless enigma.

You lay the ledger open before them, its pages filled with an entry as impossible as this realm itself. Your voice, steady and unwavering, carries through the stillness, echoing your resolve to settle the debt that binds you here.

A hush descends as you speak, and the figures regard you with a collective gaze that seems to pierce the veil between worlds. The silence stretches like a taut bowstring, each second amplifying the tension that hangs heavy in the air.

One of them steps forward, their form shimmering like moonlight on water. They extend a hand towards the ledger, and as they do so, the air around you seems to shift, the mist swirling into a vortex that threatens to pull you under its currents.

You resist the pull, your heart pounding in your chest as you hold your ground. The figure's hand hovers above the ledger for what feels like an eternity, and then, with a suddenness that startles even you, it withdraws.

The figure speaks, their voice a whisper of wind and memory, "Your debt is paid in full. But the price was high, young one. The price was your connection to the mortal world."

You feel a sudden coldness wash over you, like ice water seeping into your bones. A shiver runs through you as you realize that the unknown force that has been following you from the reeds is now gone—its bond with you severed by this transaction.

The figures turn away, their forms fading into the mist as if they were never there at all. You are left standing on the shore of Avalon, a solitary figure in a world that seems both impossibly ancient and unnaturally still.

You glance down at the ledger, now closed and empty, its pages as blank as the sky above. The dawn is approaching, the first light of dawn creeping over the horizon like a promise on the edge of fulfillment. You take one last look around, your heart heavy with both relief and loss, before turning to make your way back to the world you once called home.

As you step through the shifting space between the letters in the parchment, you feel a strange sensation—as though the weight of Avalon is lifting from your shoulders, leaving you lighter, freer than you have been in a long time. You emerge onto the shore of the mortal world, the dawn breaking over the horizon like a new beginning, and you know that whatever has passed between you and Avalon, it will never be forgotten.
