In the hush of twilight's edge, the shimmering waters of Avalon mirror an ethereal world that seems to bend and shift with each sighing breeze. Sioned, your heart pounding like a drumbeat in the stillness, approaches three figures standing at the water's edge. They are robed in hues as dark as the night itself, their features obscured by the veil of twilight.

You present them with the glowing parchment, its ink pulsating with an otherworldly light that seems to draw them near. The figures regard you for a moment, their eyes cold and unreadable as they study the artifact in your hands. With a collective nod, they acknowledge your offering. A coracle floats towards you on waves that seem to defy the wind's touch.

"A fair trade," one of them murmurs, their voice like the whispering winds themselves. "But remember, dawn breaks early here." The words hang in the air like shadows, leaving you with a sense of unease that gnaws at your insides.

As you step into the coracle, the thing lurking in the reeds grows bolder, its presence heavy and oppressive. You feel eyes upon you from all sides, a chill running down your spine as you push off from the shore. The vessel glides effortlessly across the waters, the figures watching you until they fade into the twilight mist.

The journey is not long, but it feels endless. The darkness seems to close in around you, the oppressive presence never far behind. You cling to the parchment, its light growing brighter as the darkness deepens. Finally, you reach dry land once more. The shoreline of Avalon glimmers before you, the dawn breaking over the horizon like a promise.

You step onto the shore, feeling a sense of relief wash over you. As you turn to leave, something lunges from the reeds, its form shrouded in darkness. With a cry of defiance, you brandish the parchment as a shield, the light flaring up around you like a beacon in the gathering dawn. The creature recoils, hissing and snarling as it retreats back into the shadows from which it came.

With a heavy heart, you turn your back on Avalon, knowing that this is not the end of your journey but perhaps the beginning of something much more dangerous. The light of the parchment fades as you step away, leaving you alone once more in a world that seems to hold both wonder and dread in equal measure.
