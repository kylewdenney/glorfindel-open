In the eerie twilight of Avalon, you find yourself standing before three figures who seem to embody the very essence of this land's ancient mysteries. The coracle they had arrived in is now still as a whisper on the water, its occupants focused solely on you and your offering.

As you lay the ledger upon the damp earth, each page seems to shimmer with an otherworldly glow that seeps into the parchment, leaving behind traces of arcane symbols that dance and flicker in the dim light. The figures, unmoving, regard you with a mixture of curiosity and quiet intensity.

One steps forward, her form wavering between the shadows and the firelight. She is tall, ethereal, clad in robes that shift color like the tides. Her eyes are deep pools of wisdom, filled with an ancient sadness that stirs something within you. "You have come a long way," she says, her voice echoing through the silence, "and your offering is not without merit."

She reaches out to touch the ledger, and in that moment, a gust of wind sweeps across the scene, extinguishing the fire, plunging you into darkness. The air around you grows colder, heavier, as if an unseen force is weighing upon your very soul.

"Your debt," she continues, her voice now a distant echo, "is not one of coin or deed, but one of blood and loyalty. It was inscribed by the hand of Morgause, before the time of Arthur, when this land was still young and wild. It binds your family to our service, through all generations."

As the words hang heavy in the air, you feel a sudden chill seep into your bones, as if the weight of centuries is pressing down upon you. You know that you have been given a choice — to pay this debt or face its consequences. But the terms are unclear, the stakes impossibly high.

"You have until dawn," she whispers, her form fading back into the shadows. "Choose wisely."

With that, the figures disappear, leaving you alone on the bank of the coracle pool, the ledger still clutched in your hands and the weight of a thousand years pressing down upon your shoulders. The water laps at the shore, whispering secrets to the earth, as dawn begins to break over the horizon, casting long shadows that dance upon the ancient stones.

As you stand there, contemplating your options, something stirs in the reeds behind you. A low growl echoes through the air, a reminder that you are not alone in this strange land, and that your choices will have consequences far beyond what you can imagine. The dawn is breaking, and time is running out. What will you do?
