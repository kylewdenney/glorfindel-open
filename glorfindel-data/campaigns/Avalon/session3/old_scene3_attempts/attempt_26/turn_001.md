In the waning hours of twilight, a spectral chill permeates the air as you approach the water's edge, where three ethereal figures, their forms shimmering like moonlit mist, await your arrival. The coracle they control rests gently upon the water, its surface mirroring the stars above like an otherworldly gateway.

You lay the ancient parchment before them, the mysterious words glowing faintly in the fading light. Your voice carries a resolve that echoes across the water, demanding an answer to your presence and intent. The figures regard you with a calm intensity that is both captivating and unnerving.

One of them steps forward, their form shifting subtly as they speak, "Child of Kay, we have been expecting you. You have fulfilled the prophecy foretold by Morgan le Fay, forging a path through the Veil that none have achieved before."

They pause, allowing the weight of their words to settle upon you, then continue, "Your debt is great, for you have opened a door that was once sealed. We offer you three choices: remain here in Avalon, return to your world as a changed being, or forfeit your life, ensuring the path remains closed."

As they speak, the coracle begins to ripple and shift, the water around it swirling like an otherworldly vortex. The figures gaze at you with expectant eyes, waiting for your answer in this realm of eternal twilight.
