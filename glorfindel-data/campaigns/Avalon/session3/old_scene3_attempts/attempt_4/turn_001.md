In the haunting half-light of twilight, Sioned finds herself treading a treacherous path through the reeds that tremble with each cautious step she takes. The words on her parchment pulse in time with her heartbeat, the two glowing letters seemingly beckoning her forward.

As she nears the source of the eerie movement, the reeds part to reveal a door - or rather, an archway that does not quite fit within the natural landscape. Its stones are veined with silver and woven with intricate knotwork, shimmering like moonlight on water. The air around it crackles with magic as if waiting for Sioned's touch.

The moment she places her hand upon the cold stone, the archway swings open, revealing a coracle - a small, rounded boat of woven reeds and hide - bobbing gently in the darkness. Three figures stand at its edge, their faces obscured by hooded cloaks, eyes gleaming like stars caught in mist.

Sioned's heart races as she steps forward, reciting the words on her parchment with a newfound sense of urgency. The coracle moves toward her, drawn by an unseen force. As it reaches out to touch her, Sioned hesitates - unsure whether to climb aboard or search for another way.

Suddenly, she feels a cold presence at her back, the thing in the reeds now upon her. Its breath ghosts over her neck, whispering warnings of danger and doom. Sioned's resolve hardens, and she makes her choice. With one swift motion, she leaps into the coracle and pushes off from the shore.

The boat glides smoothly through the water, guided by an otherworldly force that seems to know exactly where it is going. As they travel deeper into Avalon's heart, the reeds give way to a dense forest of twisted trees with leaves like silvered metal and bark as dark as midnight. The sky above is filled with stars so bright they seem almost within reach.

The coracle comes to rest on an island surrounded by shimmering waters. Sioned steps out onto soft, moss-covered ground and looks around, taking in the strange beauty of this mystical land. But she knows that her journey has only just begun, and that the true test lies ahead - a confrontation with forces beyond her comprehension.
